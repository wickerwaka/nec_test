## §1 CONCERN 3(b) — THE CONFOUND CONTROL ON C11

### §1.1 WHAT IS AND IS NOT ESTABLISHED

§73.9 promoted C11 — *at an INTA's T1 the chip's AD pads float and RETAIN the
previous data phase; the fabric-only HLT-sweep failures are that and nothing
else* — to a FINDING, on this inference:

* FLASH #5 (macro OFF, sitting-6 RTL) scored **146/283** in fabric;
* FLASH #6 (macro **ON**, form-2 RTL) scores **265/283** in fabric;
* the two offline `tb_sys` columns reproduce 146 and 265 on the SAME tree, and
  §73.7 measured that neither the sitting-11 IE landing nor the R7′ pass moves a
  single HLT-sweep cell in either leg.

**The inference is therefore across two bitstreams that differ by the MACRO
*and* by 90 lines of `v30u_eu.sv` *and* by a whole fit.**  The offline
measurement is what carries the "and by nothing else" — and it is offline.

### §1.2 THE TEST

run the IDENTICAL 283-cell population through `sw/x1_fabric.py` with a new leg

* **C6 not MATCH ×3** — STOP.  The board is left on a bitstream that does not

**A refutation here is REPORTED, not patched.**  Nothing in `hdl/rtl/` or `sim/`

## §2 CONCERN 4(b) — THE DISCRIMINATING CELL

### §2.1 THE PROBLEM WITH THE SITTING-11 GEOMETRY

§72.7 states the law as *"a MASKABLE recognition may not act until two clocks
after PSW.IE's RISING EDGE."*  The sitting-11 cell establishes **IE gating**
(the DEAD boundary is never taken), **maskability** (the NMI pin takes every
boundary freely), and **a floor of at least one boundary** (the rise's OWN
boundary is never taken).  It does **not** establish that the floor is counted
in CLOCKS.

On `CLI ; POPF ; NOP ; NOP` the first boundary after the rise is a couple of
clocks away, so *"permitted at ≥ 2 clocks after the rise"* and *"the first
boundary after the rise is rejected, whatever its distance"* predict the
IDENTICAL histogram.  **They coincide at that spacing and only at that
spacing.**  The landed RTL is `irq_int_lvl = int_p[2] && ie_p[2] && psw[FIE]` —
a three-clock-old pin AND a three-clock-old IE AND the LIVE IE — which is a
level test with pipelining, not a timer; it MATCHES the measurement without
being uniquely described by "2 clocks".

### §2.2 THE CELL — move the boundary, in clocks

Ten sleds, five instructions per period, in matched pairs:

| | sled | the rise |
|---|---|---|
| `pad<k>` | `CLI ; POPF ; PAD<k> ; NOP ; NOP` | IE RISES at the POPF |
| `hot<k>` | `POPF ; PAD<k> ; NOP ; NOP ; NOP` | IE already up, popped word up: **NO RISE** — the CONTROL |

`PAD<k>` ∈ { `NOP`, `CLD`, `XCHG AW,BW`, `ROL AL,CL` (CL = 3), `MUL BL` }, in
increasing clock length, all register/flag only and none of them making a bus
cycle, so `entry_frames`' three-descending-MEMW signature stays exact.

`sw/sm3_h1_cell.py run --out sm3-s13cell --variants <the ten> --pins 0,1
--waits 0,1,2,3 --delays 48 --d0 12 --hold 300` = **3,840 captures**, socket
only (`EMIT_USE_CORE` False and the module refuses to load otherwise), divider
PINNED with `div_guard`'s readback recorded, full per-clock rows + raw 64-bit
words + `SHA256SUMS`, `board_idle()` at the end, 5 consecutive transport errors
STOP the cell.

### §2.3 THE OBSERVABLES — both read off the pins, no engine

1. **WHICH BOUNDARY** — the pushed IP of the first entry, mapped to a labelled
   boundary of the sled by `s13_boundary()`: `DEAD` (after the `CLI`, IE clear),
   `OWN` (the POPF's own — where IE rose), **`B1` (the FIRST boundary after the
   rise — the one this cell moves)**, `B2`, `B3`.
2. **HOW MANY CLOCKS** — `ann_t1 − pop_t1`, from the POPF's own stack read (the
   clock at which IE is committed, on the pins) to the entry's announcement.
   This is §72.3's own coordinate.  `L_pad := min(ann−pop | B1) − min(ann−pop |
   OWN)`, **both taken on the NO-RISE control**, is the pad's clock length as
   the announcement sees it.

**THE READER IS VALIDATED ON RETAINED SILICON BEFORE ANY NEW CAPTURE.**  Run
over `sw/testdata/sm3-s11cell/` with `iepop`'s four-instruction map it
reproduces §72.3 exactly: pin 0 w0 **`OWN` 0, `DEAD` 0, `B1` 18, `B2` 6** (§72.3's
`{p3: 18, p0: 6}`) and pin 1 w0 **`OWN` 9** (§72.3's S7 = 9).  The same retained
captures give the clock coordinate on the no-rise control `iehot` at pin 0 w0:
**`OWN` 8, `B1` 12, `B2` 14, `B3` 17** — so on the NOP geometry `B1` is ~4 clocks
past the rise's own boundary, and the ±1 jitter across labels is why a LONG pad
is needed rather than a careful reading of a short one.

**WHY 48 DELAYS AND NOT SITTING 11's 24.**  The delay sweep walks the pin's
rise one clock at a time and the statistic is OCCURRENCE, so the sweep must
cover a whole sled period or a boundary can be missed for a purely GEOMETRIC
reason.  The `mul` sled's period is ~36 clocks against `nop`'s ~14.  This was
found on the ENGINE before the board (§2.6a) and it is the reason the sweep is
twice sitting 11's: **a control that fails because the sweep was too short is
not a finding about silicon.**

### §2.4 THE REGISTERED OUTCOMES — pin 0, w0, 48 captures per cell

w0 is the discriminating regime for §68.4's structural reason (at w ≥ 1 the
ordinary next-slot term grows and swallows a small floor); w1/w2/w3 are
captured and reported and carry **no** registered outcome.

| # | leg | registered |
|---|---|---|
| **P1** | CONTROL: `hot<k>` (no rise), `B1` count, every k — *the instrument can see the first boundary after the POPF at all* | **≥ 1** |
| **P1b** | CONTROL: `hot<k>` (no rise), `OWN` count, every k — *the sweep reaches the OWN window* | **≥ 1** |
| **P2** | `pad<k>`, `OWN` count, for every k with **P1b ≥ 1** | **= 0** |
| **P3** | `pad<k>`, `DEAD` count, every k | **= 0** |
| **P4** | CONTROL: `L_pad(mul)` measured on `hotmul` | **≥ 8 clocks** |
| **P5** | **THE DISCRIMINATOR** — `padmul`, `B1` count | see below |
| **P6** | NMI: `pad<k>` pin 1, `OWN` count, for every k with **P1b ≥ 1** | **≥ 1** |

**P1b IS A GEOMETRY CONTROL, NOT A LAW CONTROL, AND IT GOVERNS P2 AND P6.**
Where the no-rise control cannot reach the OWN boundary, `pad<k>`'s OWN = 0
carries no information about any law and **is not scored** for that k.  Written
down in advance because the temptation to count it is exactly the failure this
project keeps naming.

**P5, written out because it is the whole cell:**

* **`B1` ≥ 1** — a boundary sitting `L_pad(mul)` ≫ 2 clocks past the rise, and
  the FIRST boundary after it, is AVAILABLE.  The floor is counted in CLOCKS (or
  in pipeline stages of a level test, which is the same observable); **BOUNDARY
  QUANTISATION IS REFUTED** and §72.7's rendering survives as an upper bound on
  the count, not as a proven timer.
* **`B1` = 0 with `B2` ≥ 1** — the first boundary after the rise is rejected no
  matter how far away it is.  The floor is counted in BOUNDARIES;
  **§72.7's "two clocks" is REFUTED as a mechanism** and the ledger's law must
  be restated as a one-boundary rejection.
* **`B1` = 0 and `B2` = 0 and `noENTRY` = 24** — the sled admits no recognition
  at all; the cell is INCONCLUSIVE for that k and is reported as such, not
  interpreted.

**P1 = 0 for any k, or P4 < 8** — the cell's own controls failed (the instrument
cannot see the boundary, or the long pad is not long); the cell is
**INCONCLUSIVE** and nothing is concluded from P5 regardless of what it shows.

### §2.6a THE ENGINE BASELINE, MEASURED OFFLINE BEFORE THE BOARD AND BEFORE THIS FILE WAS COMMITTED

`ucore`, Verilator, the identical images and `(anchor, delay, hold=300, pin=0)`,
w0, delays 12-35 (24, the sitting-11 sweep — this run is what found §2.4's
geometry problem):

| leg | boundary histogram | `min(ann−pop)` per label |
|---|---|---|
| `padnop` | `B1` 16 · `B2` 5 · `B3` 3 — **OWN 0, DEAD 0** | B1 **11**, B2 14, B3 17 |
| `padmul` | **`B1` 24 of 24** | B1 **32** |
| `hotnop` (no rise) | **`OWN` 8** · B1 4 · B2 6 · B3 3 · B4 3 | **OWN 8**, B1 11, B2 14, B3 18, B4 20 |
| `hotmul` (no rise) | `B1` 22 · `B2` 2 — **`OWN` 0** (the geometry miss) | B1 **32**, B2 36 |

So on the ENGINE `L_pad(mul) ≈ 32 − 8 = 24 clocks`, and **the engine takes `B1`
24 times out of 24 at a distance of 24 clocks, at the same `min(ann−pop) = 32`
as the no-rise control** — i.e. the engine pays NO cost at `B1` and its rendering
is a CLOCK/level test.  `hotnop`'s ladder `OWN 8 · B1 11 · B2 14 · B3 18 · B4 20`
also reproduces the RETAINED-SILICON ladder of §2.3 (`8 · 12 · 14 · 17`) to
within the ±1 the same section names.

**This is the engine's answer, registered before silicon is asked.**  P5 is
about silicon and is not scored against it; P7 is where the two are compared.

### §2.5 P7 — THE ENGINE, AND WHAT A DIVERGENCE MEANS

`sm3_h1_cell.py s13-engine --core ucore --wait 0 --hold 300` puts the identical
images and the identical `(anchor, delay, hold, pin)` to the ucore — which
**PREDICTS**; it is handed nothing from the capture — and scores the boundary
label **cell for cell** against the chip over all **960** pin-0/pin-1 w0 captures
(10 sleds × 2 pins × 48 delays).

> **P7: registered as AGREE = 960 / 960.**

**A divergence is a REGISTERED FAILURE to REPORT, not to patch.**  No engine is
touched this sitting under any outcome of this cell.  The `sim` leg is
structurally vacuous on this observable for §4.3's reason (its directive carries
the chip's own acknowledge positions) and is **not scored**.

### §2.6 WHAT THIS CELL CANNOT DO

It cannot separate a 2-clock timer from a 3-clock one, and it is not built to:
the announcement offset jitters by ±1 across boundary labels on the retained
silicon, so the absolute count is not resolvable to one clock on this
instrument.  **The question it answers is CLOCKS vs BOUNDARIES.**  Recorded here
so that a later reading does not take a number out of it that it never carried.
