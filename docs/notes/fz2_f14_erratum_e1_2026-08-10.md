## 1. WHAT I REGISTERED, AND WHY IT WAS WRONG

The pre-registration said, at §1 and again at §5 P-3:

> A seed leaves the ledger only if its rows match *and* its architectural dump
> matches. … 14 carry a differing or absent dump (C1's 12 and C2's 2) and need
> the arch to converge as well.

and it built the floor of §6.3 (`3,671 / 3,837`) on exactly that mechanism.

**THE ARTIFACT SAYS THE ARCHITECTURAL DUMP DOES NOT ENTER THE HEADLINE AT
ALL.**  Measured on the banked corpus (`sw/testdata/campaigns/{fz2c,fz2e}/results.jsonl`,
3,840 rows) against the committed ledger:

| statement | measured |
|---|---|
| the ledger's 198 failures **are exactly** `{bad_rows != 0}` minus the 3 A-12 discards | **set equality — symmetric difference EMPTY** |
| `verdict == "SUCCESS"` ⟺ `bad_rows == 0` | **3,639 both ways** |
| matched seeds whose `arch_match` is **not** `True` | **313 of 3,639** |
| ledger failures whose `arch_match` **is** `True` | **101 of 198** |

**The ledger's `arch` column is DESCRIPTIVE, not a criterion.**  A seed with a
differing dump and clean rows is a `SUCCESS` and is not in the ledger; a seed
with a matching dump and one bad row is a failure and is.  **A rows-close is a
ledger exit, uniformly, for all 46 predicted seats — the 14 no differently from
the 32.**

Two derivation rules pinned at the same time, so that §6.6's re-derivation
control is calibrated before it is written rather than after:

    ledger first_bad_row  == results.jsonl `first_bad`              198 / 198
    ledger diverging_rows == results.jsonl `bad_rows` + `flick`     198 / 198

(The `+ flick` term is why a naive copy of `bad_rows` disagrees on 33 of the
198 by one to three rows.  The `arch` string reproduces on **196 / 198** under
`NODUMP` iff `not (done_real and done_sim)` or `mech == FORGED_DONE`, else the
differing register names excluding `MAGIC`; the two residuals — `fz2c/410067`
and `fz2e/516001`, both `OK` in the ledger — are an `OK`/`NODUMP` edge case and
are recorded, not smoothed.)

## 2. WHAT CHANGES, AND WHAT DELIBERATELY DOES NOT

**2.1 The PRIMARY headline is UNCHANGED and its basis is now uniform.**

    SEED MATCH   3,639 + 46 = 3,685 / 3,837  =  96.0386 %     failures 198 -> 152

All 46 seats close on rows or they do not; there is no second gate behind 14 of

**2.2 THE FLOOR KEEPS ITS NUMBER AND LOSES ITS RATIONALE.**  The bar stays

    >= 3,671 / 3,837  =  95.6737 %      i.e. at least 32 of the 46 seats close

**and its number is NOT moved**, because a pre-registered bar that is re-cut
after reading the artifact is not a bar.  What is corrected is what it *means*:
it is no longer "the 32 `arch = OK` seats close and the 14 arch-carrying ones
do not" — that partition has no force.  It is now simply **"at least 32 of the
46"**, an unattributed allowance, and any shortfall must be reported seat by
seat with its own cause rather than charged to a mechanism I have now shown
does not exist.

**2.3 THE REAL RESIDUAL RISK IS NAMED, AND IT WAS NOT THE ONE I NAMED.**  The
offline instruments replay the core leg in **Verilator `tb_v30_core`**; the
fabric leg is the core inside **`system_large`**.  The standing general rule
established at SM3 sitting 19 (`ucore_provenance.md` §80.B) is that **where
`tb_v30_core` and `tb_sys` disagree, fabric sides with `tb_sys` — 1,654 of
1,654 cells.**  A `tb_v30_core`-clean seat is therefore not automatically a
fabric-clean seat, and the exposure is **the same for all 46 seats**, not
concentrated on 14.  A second, smaller exposure: the rescore instruments cut
their own comparison windows (`fz2_c2_rescore` stops at the terminating NMI's
assertion row) and those are **not** the corpus's `win`, so a seat can be clean
inside the instrument's window and dirty inside the corpus's.

FREE MEASUREMENT.**  Now that `arch_match` is known to be banked per seed and
independent of the headline, the arch convergence of the trap-family seats is
worth predicting on its own:

> `arch_match` is predicted to become `True` — the differing dump is a
