## §1 FOUR CORRECTIONS TO THE COMMISSIONING BRIEF, STATED BEFORE ANY BAR

**(a) `M2` IS NOT ONE MECHANISM.  IT IS TWO, AND THE `+brktrace` TRACE
SEPARATES THEM SEED FOR SEED.**  The brief describes all 20 seats as *"the
ucore samples `brk_arm := psw[FBRK] && brk_p[BRK_FLOOR-1]` there and can only
take it one boundary later"*.  Measured on the core itself (§2), that
description is right for **ten** seats and wrong for **ten**:

* on the ten `C1`-family seats the arm is **never sampled at all** — there is
  no `BRKS` between the `TF` rise and the boundary that fires, because the
  sample rides the successor's opcode pop and an interrupt entry has no pop;
* on the ten `B2`-family seats the arm **is** sampled, at the right boundary,
  with `seen=1` — and the core then decodes `HLT` and parks, so the take never
  happens.  The floor is not involved and neither is the sample.

Folding these into one predicate would be the fitted rule the standing
principle names.  They are registered as **two laws with two bars**.

**(b) THE FLOOR IS NOT THE DEFECT ON EITHER HALF, AND THE BRIEF'S OWN
FALSIFIER SAYS SO.**  `sm3_tf_floor_cell score --core ucore --floors 4` on
this tree is **121,860 rows / 0 row-diffs, EXACT**, `W-2` survivors `{4}`,
`W-3` `iret=[2,2]` vs `popfnone=[3,3]`.  On the exemplar `fz2c/407024` the
`TF` rise is at clock **3000** and the boundary that fires is at **3006** —
**six clocks**, comfortably over a floor of 4.  `brk_seen` would have been 1
there.  Nothing in this package moves `BRK_FLOOR` or `brk_p`.

**(c) `M3` AND `M4` (i.e. ALL OF `P3`) ARE STRUCTURALLY UNSCOREABLE ON THE
ONLY OFFLINE INSTRUMENT THIS BRANCH HAS.**  `fz2_c1_rescore`'s BEFORE-leg
validation rule — *the offline first divergence must equal the board's
`first_bad_row`* — is met by **1 of 12** `M4` seats and **0 of 2** `M3` seats.
The reason is structural and was already written down in `C1`'s
pre-registration §4.2: **`M4` is the INTA-vectored delivery family, the TB has
ONE event scheduler and it is spent on the terminating NMI, so a stimulus
`INT` is never delivered offline.**  The one `M4` seat that does validate,
`fz2c/410047`, is simultaneously a member of the §64.1 counter-population that
**must not move**.  **`P3` therefore has no offline benefit instrument at all**
and no closure bar for it is registered here; what is registered is that it
must not get worse (P-5).

**(d) `sw/fz2_c1_rescore.py` HARD-CODED THE WRONG LEDGER, AND ON THIS TREE
THAT WAS NOT A COSMETIC BUG.**  `LEDGER` named
`fz2_failure_ledger_2026-08-09.json` (FLASH #13).  The FLASH #14 re-capture
REPLACED `sw/testdata/campaigns/*/captures/` in place, so **49 of the F13
ledger's 198 captures no longer exist** and the `SCOREABLE` rule was comparing
this TB against board figures taken on a different bitstream.  Repaired before
the BEFORE leg was run: the ledger is `$FZ2_LEDGER` or the lexically last
`fz2_failure_ledger*.json`, it is PRINTED, and a ledger naming a capture that
is not on disk is REFUSED rather than scored.  **`sw/b1_rescore.py:51` carries
the identical constant and is NOT touched here** — it is another package's
instrument; the defect is reported, not fixed across a territory line.

## §2 THE DIAGNOSIS, FROM THE CORE'S OWN ARM TRACE

Instrument: `sw/fz2_p2_probe.py` (new, a diagnostic and not a gate) — it
re-runs each seed's banked image through the receipted `ucore` TB with
`+brktrace` and reads the `BRKR` (TF rise) / `BRKS` (arm sample) / `BRKT`
(boundary fire) lines, whose `ce_clk` is the row index by `v30u_eu.sv`'s own
contract.  The banked capture's two legs are printed beside it.

### 2.1 MECHANISM `A` — THE SAMPLE IS SKIPPED AT A FIRING BOUNDARY

`fz2c/407024`, chip rows, both legs identical to row 3057:

    2987-2997  MEMR 69722 / 69724 / 69726        an IRET; the popped PSW
                                                 restores TF
    3001-3005  CODE 4aed9 / 4aeda                the redirect
    3015-3022  MEMR 00008 / 0000a                vector 2 — the terminating NMI
    3032-3046  MEMW 69726/69724/69722            PSW, CS, IP — pushed IP 6c79,
                                                 which is the IP the IRET just
                                                 POPPED: the NMI was taken at
                                                 the IRET's OWN retire boundary
    3052       CODE 0bf00                        one handler prefetch
    3061-3067  MEMR 00004 / 00006     chip only  THE TRAP
    3094       MEMW 6971c <- bf00                pushed IP = the handler entry

and the core's arm trace over the same clocks:

    BRKR clk=3000                      TF rises
    BRKT clk=3006 arm=0 irq=1          the boundary fires — with NO `BRKS`
                                       anywhere between 3000 and 3006

`brk_smp_n = q_pop && q_ripe && q_first` is the ONLY sampling event, and
`at_bnd` implies `!opc_valid` on all three of its arms — a boundary that
dispatches an interrupt does not pop its successor, so it never samples.  The
arm is 0 at 3006, `C1`'s landed law (`brk_arm_n = brk_arm_n && irq_take`) has
nothing to preserve, and the trap is lost.  **The distance from the rise to
the boundary is 6 clocks, so the floor is met and `brk_seen` is 1: the ONLY
missing thing is the sample.**

### 2.2 MECHANISM `B` — THE ARM IS RIGHT AND `HLT` PARKS OVER IT

`fz2e/535042`, chip rows:

    789-801    MEMR 7ddde / 7dde0 / 7dde2        an IRET; popped PSW f546,
                                                 TF set
    807-813    CODE 9aaf3 / 9aaf4                the redirect; the byte at
                                                 9aaf3 is **F4** (HLT)
    825-831    MEMR 00004 / 00006     chip only  THE TRAP — and NO HALT cycle
                                                 is ever driven
    852        MEMW 7ddde <- 0e84                pushed IP = **after** the HLT

core: `BRKR clk=805`, `BRKS clk=815 seen=1 arm:=1` — the sample is CORRECT and
at the correct boundary — and then `HALT 2aaf4` at row 820.  The core parks;
the take that was due at the next boundary never comes, because a parked part
has no next boundary.

**The pushed IP is the discriminant and it is read off the pins with no
engine: `0e84` is one past the `F4`, so silicon RETIRED the `HLT` and took the
trap at the `HLT`'s own boundary.  It did not halt and then wake.**

## §3 THE TWO LAWS — ONE TERM EACH, NO OPCODE NAMED, NO FLOP ADDED

### `L-A` — **A BOUNDARY THAT WALKS THROUGH THE EXTERNAL DOOR STILL SAMPLES THE ARM.**

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_eu.sv`, comb phase (a), the single line that defines the
sample instant:

    -   brk_smp_n = q_pop && q_ripe && q_first;
    +   brk_smp_n = (q_pop && q_ripe && q_first) || (bnd_fire && irq_take);

This is the completion of `C1`, not a second mechanism: `C1` said an external
recognition does not SPEND the arm; `L-A` says it does not SKIP THE SAMPLE
either.  The sample instant stays exactly where §85.2a measured it — **one
clock past the boundary** — and `bnd_fire` / `irq_take` are wires the same
block already reads two lines above (`if (bnd_fire) irq_fast_inta_n = …`).
**No flop, no wire, no save-state address, no BIU file, no opcode named.**

`bnd_fire && irq_take` is `at_bnd && irq_take` by construction
(`bnd_fire = at_bnd && (irq_take || brk_take)`).  The TRAP's own take is
deliberately NOT a sampling event: at a trap take `irq_take` is 0, so the five
`S_IRQ_D` sites' `brk_arm_n = brk_arm_n && irq_take` clears the arm and
nothing re-arms it.  Including it would re-sample `psw[FBRK]`, which the
entry has not yet cleared, and storm.

*`L-A′`, the fallback, taken only if `L-A` misses a registered bar and only
with its own erratum stated*: `brk_arm_n = (brk_arm_n || brk_seen) && irq_take`
at the five sites — the same law sampled on the boundary clock itself rather
than one past it.

### `L-B` — **`HLT` DOES NOT PARK WHEN IT RETIRES INTO THE TRAP.**

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_eu_step.svh`, `S_DECODE2`:

    -   if (pla3_xop(pv) == PLA3_BL1_HALT) begin
    +   if (pla3_xop(pv) == PLA3_BL1_HALT && !brk_seen) begin

`brk_seen` is the signal the **sibling arm of the same `if`, on the same
clock**, already reads for the same reason — wrfuzz W3.5's law, *"a form that
retires INTO the trap has no pop to lead"*, with its 23/23 measurement written
beside it (`v30u_eu_step.svh:176-209`): at this decode `brk_arm` is still the
previous boundary's value and `brk_seen` is the live one.  With the term
false, `PLA3_BL1_HALT` falls into the `ONE_BYTE_LOGIC` arm it is already a
member of; `v30u_eu_1bl.svh` has `default: ;` for that xop and ends with the
identical `psw_n = (psw_n & PSW_WRITABLE) | PSW_FORCED;` the HALT arm writes,
so **the fall-through executes the same PSW write and then takes the ordinary
boundary** — which is where the trap is due.  **No flop, no wire, no
save-state address, no new opcode named** (`PLA3_BL1_HALT` is already the test
being made).

### INERTNESS, BY CONSTRUCTION — THE SAME ARGUMENT `C1` USED, AND IT IS MEASURED

Both terms are gated on `PSW.TF`: `L-A` can only change a clock where
`at_bnd && irq_take && brk_seen`, `L-B` only where a `HLT` decodes with
`brk_seen`.  `C1`'s §4 measured **`PSW.TF` set in 0 of 169,843** golden cases
(`v0.1` 169,000 + the four HLT sweeps + `f4a_boundary` + `f0lock_tranche`), so
the whole golden surface is predicted **BIT-IDENTICAL, not merely no-worse**.
`sm3_tf_floor_cell` drives **no pin** with **`IE` clear**, so `irq_take` is
never 1 in it (`L-A` inert) and its sleds are `9D`/`CF`/`90` with no `F4`
(`L-B` inert).

## §6 WHAT THIS PACKAGE DELIBERATELY DOES NOT DO

* **`M3` IS BOOKED, NOT LANDED.**  The brief's term — *the arm must be spent
  by a software-`INT` entry, EU-initiated rather than pin-initiated* — is a
  one-term change with an existing discriminant (`irq_sel_brk` / `vector_fixed`
  are already the EU-vs-pin distinction), and it may well be right.  It is not
  landed **because both of its seats are OUT of the offline instrument's
  scoreable set** (§1c): a term selected on two seats and then scored on the
  same two seats it cannot even validate on is exactly the fitted rule the
  standing principle names.  *Falsifier, registered for a later sitting*: a
  capture in which the chip executes `CC`/`CD`/`CE` at a boundary with the
  BRK/TF arm standing and DOES then read vector 1 before the software
  handler's first instruction retires.  The two seats are `fz2e/517043` (`CC`)
  and `fz2e/531009` (`CD 76`).
* **`P3` / `M4` IS NOT OPENED.**  It has no offline benefit instrument (§1c).
  Its three named sub-mechanisms stay as the brief has them.  P-5 is the only
  bar written over it and it is a no-worse bar.
* **`BRK_FLOOR`, `brk_p` and the `iret`/`popfnone` asymmetry are not
  touched.**
* **No board contact, no flash.**  The board carries FLASH #14, which predates
  this landing; **no fabric figure in this repo may be quoted against this
  tree.**
* **`sim/` untouched**; `ulockstep` is informational only.
* **`nec_test.sdc` is not widened and no `r7_lint` exception is added.**
