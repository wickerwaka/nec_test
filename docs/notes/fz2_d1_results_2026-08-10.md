## 1. THE LANDING

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_biu.sv`, and nothing else in the RTL — **one term**:

    -  if (!qse_l && !(flush_rep && !flush_pend)) e_pend = 1'b1;
    +  if (!qse_l && !(flush_rep && !flush_pend && flush_direct))
    +      e_pend = 1'b1;

No flop, no save-state address, no port, no new wire, no opcode named, no case
split.  `flush_direct` already existed and the display site already read it.
The rest of the diff is the falsifier comment beside it.

**THE LAW, ONE SENTENCE.**  *The flush clock's `EMPTY` is owed to the next free
clock unless the redirect actually took the direct arbitration point.*  The
REP withdrawal decides its `EMPTY` in **two places** — the display in
`qs_e_now`, and the owe in `flush()` — and since L1 they have been reading
**different predicates**.  `flush_direct` was narrowed twice (`flush_nmi_young`
at L1, `!flush_int_live` by the `INT.F3AA` repair `9c98117a03`); the owe site
never received either narrowing and still read the L1-era stand-in
`!flush_pend`, which is `1` on **every** REP-withdrawal flush clock the golden
corpus contains.  It therefore reduced to plain `flush_rep` and **dropped** the
announcement instead of deferring it.

## 7. WHAT THIS SITTING ESTABLISHES, AND WHAT IT DOES NOT

**ESTABLISHES.**  A REP withdrawal whose flush clock cannot display `EMPTY` —
because a CODE fetch owns the queue status port — must still show it on the
next free clock, and the ucore was dropping it entirely.  The defect is that
the two sites which decide that one question had drifted apart across three
landings; one term makes the owe site read the rail the display site already
reads.  **115 of 115 golden cases close, three corpus seeds go row-clean on the
same signature, and nothing anywhere is lost or moved earlier over 2,175
corpus seed-scores and 173,000 golden cases.**  The standing evt figures
**200 / 1,200 / 200 / 1,200 / 1,200 are RESTORED**, and they have been broken
since `5403671558`.

**DOES NOT ESTABLISH.**  That `A1` is closed — §5.1 shows it is a different
mechanism and it is untouched.  Nothing about the `wr1` or `b2` corpora
(`GEN_DRIFT` on this branch, D9's reason) or the four `_v1_anchor_stop` timed
gates.  Nothing in fabric: **no board was touched and no bitstream was
flashed**, so no fabric figure may be quoted against this tree until a
re-flash.  And nothing about `flush_fast` or about dropping `!flush_pend` —
both were declined as unmeasured, with the falsifier for each written into the
RTL beside the term.
