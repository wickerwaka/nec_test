## C2-1.2 THE FALSIFIER, AND IT IS SILICON

§4.3 falsifier #2 was: *"the law is refuted by silicon if any capture shows the
CHIP running an acknowledge whose recognition can only be carried by a pin that
fell more than three clocks earlier."*

**`fz2c/404040` is that capture.**  Its INT pin is high on rows 347–646 and the
chip's acknowledge cycles are at `T1` rows **358, 365, 654, 664** — two entries,
and the second one's first `INTA` `T1` is at **654, seven clocks after the pin
fell.**  Traced on the baseline core, the two legs are in lockstep to row 650
and the ucore's recognition there is carried by `intr_pending` **alone**
(`int_p[2]` = the pin at 647 = 0).  **Silicon takes it.  The registered form
deletes it, and that is the lost seed.**

**AND IT IS NOT A SINGLETON.**  Engine-free, chip rows only, over all **252**
INT-stimulus captures: **31 of the 251 chip acknowledge runs begin at or after
the pin's own fall**, at `+0 … +40` clocks.  Twenty-seven of those are the
`hold = 2` population, where a 2-clock pulse makes it unavoidable; **four are
`hold = 300`**, in three seeds — `fz2c/405056` (+0), `fz2c/404040` (+7) and
`fz2c/405040` (+18 and +40).  *A maskable request outlives its pin on this
part, and by more than the pipeline's depth.*

## C2-1.4 THE REPLACEMENT — **THE LATCH MAY SUBSTITUTE FOR THE PIN ONLY WHILE THE FLOOR IS MATURING**, WHICH IS WHAT ITS COMMENT ALREADY SAID

`v30u_eu.sv`'s own text, from `7647e604e0` and unchanged since:

> *"A request sampled on the same clock IE rises is retained in the existing
> `intr_pending` latch (block (a)) **until this floor matures**."*

The floor is three clocks.  `intr_pending` is cleared **only by an interrupt
entry**, so the retention outlives the floor by however long the next boundary
takes to arrive — 215 and 289 clocks on two of the nine census rows.  **The
repair is to make the sentence true**:

```
-wire irq_int_lvl = (int_p[2] ||
-                    (intr_pending && (rep_kind == REP_NONE))) &&
-                   ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];
+wire irq_int_lvl = (int_p[2] ||
+                    (intr_pending && (rep_kind == REP_NONE) && !ie_p[3])) &&
+                   ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];
```

**ONE TERM.  No flop, no new state, no save-state address, no opcode named, and
no register that did not already exist.**  `!ie_p[3]` is this module's OWN
idiom for exactly this meaning, already written down beside `eu_bnd_post`:
*"`!ie_p[3]` is exactly 'the IE gate is what held this boundary', since the take
needs `ie_p[2]` and IE was still low four clocks back."*  A latch armed at clock
`A` is readable at `A+1 … A+3` and dead from `A+4`, because `ie_p[3]` is IE four
clocks ago.  Nothing else in the module moves; `intr_pending` keeps its setter,
its REP-withdrawal writer, its `C_INTR` reader, its flop and its address.
