## 1. THE LANDING

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_biu.sv`, one wire, and nothing else in the RTL:

    -wire flush_staged_eval = flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_int_live &&
    -                         !r_wr_done_p && flush_idle;
    +wire flush_staged_eval = flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_src_live &&
    +                         flush_idle;

**No flop added or removed.  `v30u_ss_pkg.sv` and the two ss `.svh` files
untouched.  No new port, no EU output, no opcode named, no table, no per-seed
case.  The expression SHRINKS by one term.**

It is the second half of A1.  A1 gave the DISPLAY the recognition rail
(`flush_src_live = flush_int_live || flush_nmi`) and deliberately left the
ARBITRATION consumer reading the maskable package pin alone; this gives the
arbiter the same rail, so the two places that decide a REP withdrawal finally
decide it the same way.  The fitted one-clock lookback `!r_wr_done_p` is
DELETED rather than re-fitted — silicon falsifies it 2 for 2 on the only
clocks in the corpus that exercise it.

**DECLARED SIDE EFFECT, taken deliberately**: that term was `r_wr_done_p`'s
only functional consumer, so the flop is now driven, reset and saved but read
only by the save-state mux.  Its flop and its SSA address are LEFT IN PLACE —
retiring one is the save-state owner's call and `ss_lint` had to stay UNMOVED.
**Booked for the ss owner, not taken here.**  (The comment beside it originally
named the SSA symbol and `ss_lint` FAILED on the third occurrence — the lint
working, and it caught a comment.)
