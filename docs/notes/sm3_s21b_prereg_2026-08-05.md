## §1 THE MECHANISM — **F57: A READ'S COMPLETION CLOCK IS STAMPED AT THE
## CYCLE'S OWN EVAL, WHICH IS WHERE EVERY OTHER EVAL-KEYED QUANTITY ALREADY IS**

### §1.1 The measurement

`s10-hltsweep-w0 HLT.INT`, the model's own commit trace (`V30SIM_FLUSHTRACE`),
across the failing cell and its two passing neighbours:

| cell | INTA1 display | INTA1 T1 | INTA2 display, **model** | **golden** |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `idx 5` — **FAILS** | 17 | **19** (= disp + 2) | **25** | **24** |
| `idx 6` — passes | 18 | 19 (= disp + 1) | 25 | 25 |
| `idx 7` — passes | 19 | 20 (= disp + 1) | 26 | 26 |

**Silicon's second acknowledge is `display + 7`.  The model's is `T1 + 6`.**
They are the same number for every cycle whose T1 opens the clock after its
display, and they part only where the T1 WAITED — which in this whole corpus is
the acknowledge that follows a woken HALT, and nothing else.  That is M22's own
sentence about `cmt_expire_`, one mechanism over.

### §1.2 Where the clock is lost, exactly

`wait_next_read()` blocks in two stages: *until a completion stamp EXISTS*, then
*until the clock the stamp names*.  The stamp is `rd_done_q_.push_back(e + 2)`
and it is executed in the `ci_ == last_i` (T4) block — but its VALUE is
`e + 2`, and `e` is the cycle's EVAL instant, which M22's second consequence
(`eval_i = disp + 3 - c` at w0) already places at `disp + 3`.

On `idx 5` the eval is at clock 20, the stamp names **22**, and it is not
pushed until the T4 tick at clock 22 — so the wait cannot leave before **23**,
one clock after the instant the model itself computed.  Traced:

```
WNR 16 t=22 -> 23      the wait wanted 22 and left at 23
POST 24                ...so the second acknowledge is posted at 24
```

On `idx 6` the eval is at 21, the stamp names 23, T4 is at 22 and the wait
leaves at 23 — the two coincide, and the model is right.

> **F57.**  A read's completion clock is stamped **at the cycle's own eval**,
> not at its T4.  The value is unchanged (`e + 2`); what moves is the clock the
> EU can first SEE it on.

**W0-NEUTRAL BY CONSTRUCTION for every ordinary cycle**: at w0 the eval is at
index 2 and T4 at index 3, so the stamp becomes visible one clock earlier and
the wait's second stage (`clk_ < e + 2`) is unchanged — the EU still leaves at
`e + 2` either way.  Under waits `eval_i == last_i` and the two sites are the
SAME CLOCK.  The change can only bite where the eval and T4 are separated by
more than one clock, i.e. where the display waited.

### §1.3 The change

* **`sim/`** — push the read completion (and `last_wval_`) in the `ci_ ==
  eval_i` block instead of the `ci_ == last_i` block.  No new law, no constant,
  no per-case anything; one `bool` guards the double-push when
  `eval_i == last_i`.
* **the `ucore`** — the same edge, in `v30u_biu.sv`'s own eval/T4 blocks.
