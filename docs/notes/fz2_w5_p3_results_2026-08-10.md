## 0. HEADLINE

The one-predicate law the prereg registered — suspend the prefetcher at a
**vectoring HALT wake**, `hlt_wake_int && irq_int_lvl`, releasing on the
vector's `q_flush` — **did not close the 3 C2 seats and BROKE the §64.1 sharp
control `fz2c/404040` (bad 0 → 1438).**  It is REVERTED.  The mechanism is real
(the seats improve, the escaping wake-edge prefetch is a true divergence) but
the **carrier is wrong**: `irq_int_lvl` at the wake does not separate the seats
where silicon SUSPENDS from the seats where silicon RUNS the acknowledge late,
and those two populations are **§64.1-identical on every coordinate in the
recognition path**.  This is the wall §64.1 and wave-4's C-β both named; wave-5
now has it MEASURED on a landed candidate rather than argued.

wire eu_halt_wake_susp = hlt_wake_int && irq_int_lvl;   // the vectoring wake

## 3. THE MECHANISM OF THE REFUTATION, NAMED

The escaping prefetch is real: `+utrace` on `fz2c/404071` shows a `ps=CODE`
fetch granted at the wake while `suspended = 0`, one-to-three clocks before the
INTA microcode's own `I_SUSP` raises it; the chip drives `fetch → one idle →
INTA` and the core drives that extra cycle and delivers ~8 rows late.  Suspending
at the wake DOES suppress it — which is why the three seats' `bad` fell ~8-11 %.

But `irq_int_lvl` is true at the wake of **both**:

* the 3 C2 seats, where silicon SUSPENDS (no wake prefetch), and
* `fz2c/404040` / the c2a "`run − arm == 2`" seats, where **silicon RUNS the
  acknowledge late** and the wake-edge fetch (or its absence) is timed by the
  sub-clock IE-rise / pin-fall race, NOT by the recognition level.

So the term fires on the second population and INTRODUCES divergence there
(`fz2c/404040` 0 → 1438).  It also introduces a NEW earlier divergence on the
seats themselves (first 244 → 241), so even where it helps the later rows it is
not the die's clean rule.  This is exactly the separation §64.1 records as
unreachable: *"`fz2c/404040` — where SILICON RUNS THE ACKNOWLEDGE, seven clocks
after its own pin fell — is identical to [the c2a seats] on every coordinate in
the recognition path.  Separating those needs a directed board cell on the
IE-rise / pin-fall race, and there is not one"* (`v30u_eu.sv` §827-832).

## 4. WHAT IS BOOKED (the precise blocker for the next attempt)

**C-γ — the vectoring-HALT-wake prefetch suspend is REAL but has NO OFFLINE
CARRIER that separates it from the §64.1 acknowledge-timing population.**  The
suspend must fire on a wake that suspends and NOT on one where silicon runs the
acknowledge late, and the only quantity that differs between them is the
sub-clock phase of the IE-rise against the pin-fall — which `irq_int_lvl`,
`hlt_wake_int`, `int_p[*]` and `ie_p[*]` all read at clock granularity and
cannot resolve.

*What blocks it, so the next sitting does not re-discover it*: any predicate
built from the recognition level (`irq_int_lvl` / `irq_take` / `bnd_take`) at
the wake fires on `fz2c/404040` and the c2a "`run − arm == 2`" seats and breaks
them; a predicate that excludes them by naming `run − arm` is the fitted
two-case rule §64.1 forbids (it would be chosen by reading these seats and
scored on them).  The naive HLT-decode and `halted`-route forms are already
refuted by wave-4.

*Owner*: the sub-clock IE-rise / pin-fall race at a `sti; hlt` wake.
*Falsifier / re-open condition*: a DIRECTED BOARD CELL on that race — the same
cell §64.1 and wave-4's C-β both say does not exist — that measures, on
silicon, whether the wake prefetch is present as a function of the IE/pin phase.
With that cell the seats and `fz2c/404040` can be separated by a MEASURED phase
threshold rather than a fitted one, and C-γ re-opens keyed on it.

*Also booked, unchanged from wave-4*: C-α′ (the B2 seats' flushable
prefetch-suspend) and C-β (the S_HALTED park cost) — this wave neither closes
nor disturbs them (p3 B2 seats UNMOVED).
