## §2 H7 — THE NMI RECOGNITION FLOOR, PRIMED vs DRY

### §2.1 What is already established, and what is blocked

§63.2, over the 208 banked `evt.pin = 1` seeds (all `hold = 2`):

| | chip | `ucore` | `sim` |
|---|---|---|---|
| minimum vector-read T1 measured from the assert clock `A` | **A + 12** | A + 13 | A + 13 |

and, restricted to seeds with no earlier divergence, the split is total:
**14 seeds at the chip's floor, ucore exact on 0 of 14; 147 at ≥ 13, exact on
144.**  §63.3 then built the obvious one-register fix (the NMI pin pipeline
4 → 3) and **it is REFUTED**: 17 golden NMI cases break and they are exactly
the 17 the EVTTRACE census calls A-limited.  Inside the bank the same
disagreement appears — of the 25 clean banked NMI seeds with `B ≤ A + 3`,
**14 fire at `A + 3` and 7 at `A + 4`** — and the wait class, the rig delay,
the bus owner at A and the queue occupancy at A were all tabulated and all four
cut across the split.

**H7 is BLOCKED.  This cell exists to unblock it or to say what still blocks
it.**

### §2.2 The stimulus, verbatim from §63.3

One fixed instruction stream; the assert delay swept **one clock at a time** so
`A` walks past a fixed boundary; waits 0/1/2/3; the whole sweep run **twice**,
once with the queue **PRIMED** and once **DRY**.

| sweep | stimulus | why |
|---|---|---|
| **primed** | `nop` — a NOP sled | the prefetcher runs far ahead, so every recognition boundary is reached with bytes already in the queue |
| **dry** | `ebnext` — a chain of `EB 00` near JMPs | every instruction flushes, so every boundary is reached on a COLD FETCH — the golden A-limited cases' own shape |

Both are the **sitting-2 H1 cell's own stimuli**, imported from
`sm3_h1_cell.image_of` rather than re-written, so nothing new is introduced
beside the question.  `hold = 2` on every capture, because every banked NMI
seed carries `hold = 2`.  Delays `d = 4 … 23` (20 per cell), 2 variants × 4
waits × 20 delays = **160 captures**.

**The observable**: `A = arm + delay + 2` (the rig's own assert clock,
established four independent ways in §63.1) and `V` = the T1 of the chip's NMI
vector read (`MEMR` at `0x00008`).  Sweeping the delay one clock at a time
walks A past a fixed boundary, so `V − A` falls by 1 per step while the
recognition is B-limited and then **stops falling**.  *The value it stops at is
the floor, and that knee is the whole measurement.*

### §2.3 MEASURED OFFLINE FIRST, so the engine leg is not chosen after the fact

Run before any board contact, through the Verilator `ucore` on exactly these
images and this instrument (`sm3_h7_cell.measure`), w0, delays 4…25:

| sweep | ucore floor `min(V − A)` |
|---|---|
| `nop` (primed) | **13** |
| `ebnext` (dry) | **13** |

The engine's floor is **uniform at 13 across both queue states**, which is
§63.2's figure reproduced on this stimulus.  So the cell's discriminating
question is entirely about the CHIP.

### §2.4 THE PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS

* **Q1 — RIG INTEGRITY / THE STANDING FALSIFIER.**  No capture shows
  `V − A < 12`.  §63.3 registered *"a banked NMI seed whose vector read opens
  earlier than `A + 12`"* as H7's falsifier; a directed cell below 12
  falsifies the floor outright, and it **STOPS the cell** for re-examination
  rather than being averaged in.
* **Q1b — THE SWEEP MUST HAVE A KNEE.**  Within each (variant, wait) cell the
  measured `V − A` takes at least two distinct values across the delay sweep.
  A gap that is CONSTANT across all 20 delays would mean `A` is not the
  coordinate it is taken to be; that is a **rig-integrity FINDING** and it
  stops the cell.
* **Q2 — THE ENTRY-SIDE READING.**  The chip's floor **DIFFERS** between the
  primed and the dry sweep (one 12, the other 13), at one or more wait levels.
  *Then the extra clock is on the ENTRY (bus) side, the NMI latch depth is
  right, both engines are wrong in the same one place, and the fix is a
  queue-state-dependent entry term.*  **A landing is authorised by this
  outcome and only by it**, sim first, under §2.6's bars.
* **Q3 — THE LATCH READING (H7 STAYS BLOCKED).**  The chip's floor is **12 in
  BOTH sweeps** at every wait level.  Then the queue state does not select,
  the pin pipeline really is one clock shorter than both engines model it —
  **and §63.3's golden refutation still stands**, so the two silicon
  populations remain in contradiction and NOTHING IS LANDED.  The cell's
  deliverable in this branch is the report of which axes still separate the
  chip's floor cells from its non-floor cells (bus owner and T-state index at
  A, queue occupancy at V), banked for the next hypothesis.
* **Q4 — INCONCLUSIVE, and it is stated because it is possible.**  The chip's
  floor is **13 in both sweeps**.  Then this stimulus never reaches the floor
  the bank reaches; the cell is inconclusive about where `A + 12` lives and is
  reported as such.  **It is NOT a refutation of §63.2** — those 30 seeds are
  banked silicon, and a directed cell that fails to reproduce a condition has
  not disproved it.

### §2.5 WHAT WOULD FALSIFY A Q2 LANDING BEFORE IT IS TAKEN

A queue-state-dependent entry term must reproduce, without exception:

* every chip cell in this sweep, both variants, all four wait levels;
* the **17 golden A-limited NMI cases** §63.3 identifies — they are cold-fetch
  cases, so a term keyed on the queue state must leave them where they are;
* the **14-at-`A+3` / 7-at-`A+4`** in-bank split, whose discriminating variable
  is unknown.  **If the term does not explain that split it is not the
  mechanism**, and a landing that merely moves the score is refused.

## §3 H3 CLASS B — THE GRANT-ORDER SWAP

### §3.1 What is already established

§63.5 split `PF_LOST` in two: **class A** is the 8080 landing pad (92 ucore /
88 sim, already booked, not arbitration at all) and **class B** is the
arbitration residue — **221 sim / 37 ucore**, with **`delta = 0` on 184 of
221**.  Both sides open a cycle on the SAME CLOCK and disagree about whose it
is: a **grant-order swap**, not a timing slip.  §63.6 tested the one candidate
the work order named and reported it as a **NEGATIVE result**: the contested
slots skew to a fuller queue (186 of 221 at occ 3-5, against a control
dominated by occ 2) but occ 0 through 5 all occur, so there is no single
threshold and M4's `occ + inflight ≤ 4` is not off by one.

**Class B is 184-of-221 a `sim/` debt**, so any landing out of this cell is
sim-first.

### §3.2 The stimulus, and how the queue is pre-loaded

There is no queue-preload port on the socket, so the occupancy is set the way
the part sets it.  Every loop body is

```
    EB 00            ; near JMP to the next instruction -- FLUSHES the queue
    NOP  x PAD       ; PAD = 0 .. 7 filler bytes
    <the EU access>  ; at a fixed data address, so the address is never a
                     ;   confound with the stimulus
```

The flush starts a cold refill at a known instant and `PAD` separates it from
the EU access, so the prefetcher has run ahead by a different, monotone amount
when the EU asks for the bus.  **The achieved occupancy is never assumed** — it
is MEASURED off the capture's own `QS` port at every EU access, by §63.6's own
counting rule (bytes delivered by CODE cycles completed since the last `QS = E`,
minus the pops seen since).

Cell (a) is the PAD sweep.  Cell (b) is the pair of variants that move the EU's
request instant either side of the baseline:

| variant | the access | the request instant |
|---|---|---|
| `mw` | `C6 06 disp16 ib` — `MOV byte [disp16], imm8` | the baseline.  A MEMW, which is 106 of the model's 221 class-B cells |
| `mwseg` | `26` + the same | an ES: override is decoded before the EA is formed, so the EU's request arrives **LATER**, with the prefetcher's eligibility unchanged |
| `mr` | `A0 disp16` — `MOV AL, [disp16]` | an immediate displacement with no ModRM to decode, so the request arrives **EARLIER** |

3 variants × 8 pads × 4 waits = **96 captures**.  **No pin event**: this cell
is pure bus arbitration and `evt` is None.

### §3.3 MEASURED OFFLINE FIRST — the sweep is real before the board sees it

Through the C++ model on exactly these images, w0, the ACHIEVED occupancy at
the EU access's T1 across `PAD` 0…7 spans **{2, 3, 4, 5}** in all three
variants (e.g. `mw`: pad 7 → occ 2, pad 0 → occ 3 and 5, pad 4 → occ 5), and
the model's own grant order is **prefetch-first on 100 % of accesses in every
cell**.  So the stimulus does move the coordinate cell (a) asks about, and the
model has no variation of its own to confound the chip's.

### §3.4 THE PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS

* **R1 — THE SWEEP IS REAL ON THE CHIP.**  Across `PAD` 0…7 the chip's
  measured occupancy at the EU access spans **≥ 3 distinct values** in each
  variant.  If it spans fewer, cell (a) cannot be answered by this stimulus and
  the cell **says so** rather than reporting the narrow result as an answer.
* **R2 — GRANT PRIORITY AS A FUNCTION OF OCCUPANCY ALONE.**  Pooled over `PAD`,
  within each (variant, wait, occupancy) cell the chip's grant order is
  **CONSTANT**.  *If the same occupancy yields both orders inside one
  variant/wait, occupancy alone does not decide the tie-break and §63.6's
  negative result is CONFIRMED on directed data* — which is a real finding and
  is reported as one.
* **R3 — WHICH OF THE TWO READINGS.**  With the request instant moved later
  (`mwseg`) and earlier (`mr`):
  * if the chip-vs-engine agreement curve **SHIFTS with the request instant**,
    the reading is *"the engine's EU request is at the wrong clock"* — class
    A's own `delta = +2` shape, and the mechanism is a microcode latency, not
    an arbitration priority;
  * if it **STAYS PUT and tracks occupancy only**, the reading is *"the chip's
    tie-break is prefetch-first"* and the mechanism is in the arbiter.
* **R4 — THE FAMILY MUST APPEAR.**  At least one directed capture reproduces
  §63.6's signature: the chip and the engine open a cycle on the **same clock**
  and disagree about **whose** it is.  **If ZERO appear across the 96 captures,
  the cell has not reached the family**, and that is the reported result — the
  banked family then needs a stimulus this one does not contain, and no
  conclusion about the tie-break may be drawn from these captures.

### §3.5 DISPOSITION

**Bank and book.**  A landing is taken **only if a single mechanism emerges**,
is pre-registered before it is written, and is **sim-first** (class B is
184-of-221 a `sim/` debt).  §2.6's must-not-move and must-not-fall lists apply
unchanged to any such landing.  A many-cased rule, a per-occupancy table or a
per-variant special case is **not** a deliverable — the standing principle
names a fitted table as a signal of misunderstanding, and §63.6 already
reported that no single occupancy threshold exists.
