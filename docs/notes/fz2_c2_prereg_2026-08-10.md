## §1 THE BRIEF IS CORRECTED AGAINST THE ARTIFACT, BEFORE ANYTHING ELSE

The brief and `fz2_corpus_prereg_2026-08-08.md` §38.6's `C2` entry are right
about the population and **wrong about the mechanism and about the partition.**

**(a) CONFIRMED, exactly as written.**  12 seeds; 11 banked `FUNCTIONAL`, one
`KNOWN_ACCEPTED` (`fz2c/410047`); 11 soup / 1 raw; `open_bus` on 1 of 12.  The
INTA-cycle counts are chip **2** / core **4** on **five** seeds
(`fz2c/405013`, `fz2c/405072`, `fz2e/511030`, `fz2e/512056`, `fz2e/516038`)
and chip **4** / core **6** on **two** (`fz2c/405002`, `fz2e/513019`).

**(b) ERRATUM — the remainder is FIVE seeds, not four.**  §38.6 accounts for
*"chip 4 / core 3 on two seeds, chip 2 / core 0 and 2/1 on one each"* = 4, and
7 + 4 = 11.  The twelfth is **`fz2e/516065`, which runs 2 INTA cycles on BOTH
legs** — its divergence is *when* the acknowledge is issued (17 clocks late),
not how many there are.

**(c) ERRATUM — the family does NOT share one law.  It is a THREE-WAY
partition**, established below on the rows and on the ucore's own recognition
state:

| sub | n | seeds | what parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| **C2a** | **7** | `fz2c/405002` `fz2c/405013` `fz2c/405072` `fz2e/511030` `fz2e/512056` `fz2e/513019` `fz2e/516038` | the core runs **one extra ENTRY** (two extra INTA cycles), and it is carried by `intr_pending` with the pin already released |
| **C2b** | **4** | `fz2c/404071` `fz2e/514044` `fz2e/516001` `fz2e/516065` | the recognition is on time and the **first acknowledge cycle is issued LATE** (+7 / +9 / +16 / +17 clocks).  Three of the four are a **HALT wake** (a `HALT` status is the last cycle before the divergence on all three); the fourth is a **REP withdrawal** (`rep_kind = 3` at the divergence) |
| **C2c** | **1** | `fz2c/410047` | the core **never recognises at all** — `irq_int_lvl` is low for the whole 4,200-clock run.  Its stimulus hold is **2 clocks**, it is the only raw-tier and only non-`FUNCTIONAL` member, and it is one of the four seeds §38.6 already named as the `C1` counter-population (core vector-1 count 7 vs chip 0) |

**(d) THE MECHANISM IS NOT THE ONE THE BRIEF NAMES.**  It is not *"the
re-recognition window after an acknowledge is one boundary too wide"*, and it is
not the brief's *"acknowledges on BOTH edges of something silicon acknowledges
once"*: the doubling is chip `N` → core `N + 2` because **one whole extra ENTRY**
runs, and an entry is two INTA cycles.  There is no per-edge double-acknowledge
anywhere in the 12.

## §2 THE DIAGNOSIS — **`intr_pending` HAS NO EXPIRY, AND IT STANDS IN FOR THE PIN AFTER THE PIN IS GONE**

### 2.1 The instrument

`tb_v30_core` has ONE pin-event scheduler; the board rig has three and every
fz2 capture uses at least two (the seed's STIMULUS on scheduler 0, the
campaign's TERMINATING NMI on `TERM_SCHED`).  `sw/fz2_a1_rescore.py` spends the
TB's scheduler on the TERMINATOR — **so the stimulus pin is never asserted
there and this family is invisible to it.**  This sitting adds
**`sw/fz2_c2_rescore.py`**, which spends it on the STIMULUS and cuts the
comparison window at the terminating NMI's own assertion row
(`term.hold_rows.pin_nmi[-1][0]`), a rule fixed before any result was seen.

**THE CONTROL, and it is what makes the instrument readable**: on all twelve
`C2` seeds it reproduces the **BANKED first-divergence row exactly** — 244,
527, 1331, 636, 227, 611, 1475, 589, 235, 584, 1265, 472 — with the banked
first-divergence signature.

The recognition state was read with a **temporary** `+c2dbg` probe in
`hdl/tb/tb_v30_core.sv` (`dut.u_eu.intr_pending`, `int_p`, `ie_p`, `psw[FIE]`,
`nmi_latch`, `st`, `irq_int_lvl`, `rep_kind`, one line per recorded clock).
**The probe is REVERTED and is not part of this landing**; the tree at this
commit does not contain it.

### 2.2 The law, in the RTL's own terms

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_eu.sv`:

```
wire irq_int_lvl = (int_p[2] ||
                    (intr_pending && (rep_kind == REP_NONE))) &&
                   ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];
```

and, in block (a) of the clocked step:

```
if (ie_now && !ie_p_n[0] && int_p[0])
    intr_pending_n = 1'b1;
```

`intr_pending` is set **on the clock IE rises, if the pin was up one clock
earlier**, and it is cleared **only by an interrupt entry**
(`v30u_eu_step.svh:838`).  Its comment says it is retained *"until this floor
matures"* — three clocks.  **Its implemented lifetime is unbounded.**

### 2.3 The rows

Worked exemplar `fz2c/405072`, both legs, one clock per row:

| clock | chip | core | `intr_pending` | `int_p` | `ie_p` | `psw[FIE]` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 628 | pin **1** | — | 0 | `1111` | `1111` | 0 |
| **629** | pin **0** — the pin FALLS | — | 0 | `1111` | `0000` | **1** — IE RISES on the same clock |
| 630 | — | — | **1** | `1110` | `0001` | 1 |
| 632 | `CODE` | `CODE` | 1 | `1000` → **`int_p[2] = 0`** | `0111` → `ie_p[2] = 1` | 1 |
| 636 | `Ti CODE` | **`Ti INTA`** | 0 (the entry cleared it) | | | |
| 637 | `T1 CODE 48106` | **`T1 INTA`** | | | | |

At clock 632 `irq_int_lvl` is high **solely because of `intr_pending`** —
`int_p[2]` is the pin at clock 629, and the pin is gone.  Silicon does not
enter.  The core does, four clocks later, and that is the whole divergence.

### 2.4 It holds on all seven, and the arithmetic is the pipeline's own depth

For each `C2a` seed: the chip's INT-pin window, the clocks at which the ucore's
`irq_int_lvl` is carried by `intr_pending` **alone** (`int_p[2]` low), the
core's extra INTA `T1` rows, and the chip's INTA `T1` rows:

| seed | pin window | latch-carried run | pin fall + 3 | core INTA `T1` | chip INTA `T1` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2c/405002` | 220…**520** | 523–524 | **523** | 231 238 379 388 **528 537** | 231 238 379 388 |
| `fz2c/405013` | 1026…**1326** | 1329–1330 | **1329** | 1037 1044 **1334 1343** | 1037 1044 |
| `fz2c/405072` | 329…**629** | 632–633 | **632** | 398 411 **637 650** | 398 411 |
| `fz2e/511030` | 273…**573** | 576–612 | **576** | 282 292 **615 625** | 282 292 |
| `fz2e/512056` | 1166…**1466** | 1469–1470 | **1469** | 1179 1190 **1476 1487** | 1179 1190 |
| `fz2e/513019` | 267…**567** | 570–585 | **570** | 278 287 437 444 **590 599** | 278 287 437 444 |
| `fz2e/516038` | 627…**927** | **1209–1257** | 930 | 649 663 **1266 1276** | 649 663 |

**Six of the seven open the latch-carried window at EXACTLY `pin fall + 3`** —
`int_p`'s own depth — which is the arithmetic statement that the latch is
standing in for `int_p[2]` the instant the pipeline drains.  The seventh,
`fz2e/516038`, opens it **282 clocks after the pin fell**, because IE was down
in between: that seed is the demonstration that the lifetime is unbounded and
not merely three clocks too long.

On all seven the chip's INTA sequence is a **strict prefix** of the core's, and
the extra pair is the FIRST divergence.

**AND THE FOUR `C2b` SEEDS AND `C2c` HAVE NO LATCH-CARRIED RECOGNITION AT
ALL** (`fz2c/404071`, `fz2e/514044`, `fz2e/516001`, `fz2e/516065`: empty;
`fz2c/410047`: `irq_int_lvl` never high).  That is why they are a different
mechanism and why this landing must leave them exactly where they are.

### 4.5 The edit, and the attribution control

**THE EDIT (registered form, "both halves"):** delete the two lines
`5403671558` added, restoring §72.5's board-authorised law:

```
-wire irq_int_lvl = (int_p[2] ||
-                    (intr_pending && (rep_kind == REP_NONE))) &&
-                   ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];
+wire irq_int_lvl = int_p[2] && ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];

-        if (ie_now && !ie_p_n[0] && int_p[0])
-            intr_pending_n = 1'b1;
```

**WHY BOTH HALVES AND NOT JUST THE DISJUNCT.**  Deleting the disjunct alone
leaves the setter writing a latch whose only remaining reader is the REP
withdrawal's `C_INTR` — and, because the spurious entry that used to CLEAR it
no longer happens, it would sit set for longer than it does today and could arm
a REP withdrawal that silicon does not take.  That is a NEW hazard introduced
by a partial fix.  Deleting the setter alone leaves a disjunct that can only be
reached from the REP arm, i.e. dead-but-live logic on the recognition path.
Deleting both is the exact revert of ONE named mechanism to the ONE form that
has a directed board measurement behind it.  `intr_pending` keeps its older
REP-withdrawal writer (`v30u_eu_cond.svh`), its `C_INTR` reader, its flop and
its save-state address; nothing else in the module moves.

**ATTRIBUTION CONTROL (run after the primary, on the 12 `C2` seeds only):**
build a variant with the **setter deleted and the disjunct KEPT**.  If the 7
close there too, the carrier is the IE-rise setter and the disjunct is inert on
this population; if they do not, the disjunct is carrying it from the REP arm.
Either answer is recorded.  The control is **not landed**.
