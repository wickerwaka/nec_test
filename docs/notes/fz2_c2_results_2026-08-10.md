## 1. HEADLINE

**The brief's `C2` is not one family and not the mechanism it names.**  It is
**7 + 4 + 1**, the seven are one law, and the law is not *"the re-recognition
window after an acknowledge is one boundary too wide"* — it is that
**`intr_pending`, the latch that remembers a request present when IE rises, has
no expiry.**

**The registered form of the fix — delete the retention — was built, scored and
REFUTED BY ITS OWN FALSIFIER**: silicon *does* acknowledge a request whose pin
has already gone (`fz2c/404040`, chip acknowledge at row 654 with the pin down
since 647).  It was reverted.

**What landed is ONE TERM**, `&& !ie_p[3]`, which makes the latch's own
twelve-month-old comment — *"retained … until this floor matures"* — true.
**Two seats close, one improves, four are left OPEN and NAMED, and ZERO seeds
are lost anywhere.**

## 2. THE LANDING

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_eu.sv`, and nothing else in the RTL:

```
-wire irq_int_lvl = (int_p[2] ||
-                    (intr_pending && (rep_kind == REP_NONE))) &&
-                   ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];
+wire irq_int_lvl = (int_p[2] ||
+                    (intr_pending && (rep_kind == REP_NONE) && !ie_p[3])) &&
+                   ie_p[2] && psw[FIE];
```

**No flop added or removed.  No save-state address.  No new state.  No opcode
named.  No register introduced** — `ie_p[3]` already exists and this module
already uses it for exactly this meaning, beside `eu_bnd_post`: *"`!ie_p[3]` is
exactly 'the IE gate is what held this boundary', since the take needs
`ie_p[2]` and IE was still low four clocks back."*  A latch armed at clock `A`
is readable at `A+1 … A+3` and dead from `A+4`: the three-clock floor, and
nothing longer.

`intr_pending` keeps its IE-rise setter, its older REP-withdrawal writer
(`v30u_eu_cond.svh`), its `C_INTR` reader, its flop and its address.

## 3. THE INSTRUMENT — `sw/fz2_c2_rescore.py`, NEW, AND IT IS WHY THE FAMILY WAS DIAGNOSABLE AT ALL

`tb_v30_core` has ONE pin-event scheduler; every fz2 capture uses at least two.
`sw/fz2_a1_rescore.py` spends the TB's scheduler on the campaign's TERMINATING
NMI — correct for a family reached without a pin, and it means **the seed's own
stimulus pin is never asserted there, so `C2` is invisible to it.**  369 of the
725 retained captures carry a stimulus event (252 INT, 117 NMI).

`fz2_c2_rescore` spends the scheduler on the STIMULUS and cuts the comparison
window at `min(banked win, the terminating NMI's own assertion row)` — a rule
fixed before any result was seen.  **CONTROL: on all twelve `C2` seeds it
reproduces the BANKED first-divergence row and signature exactly** (244, 527,
1331, 636, 227, 611, 1475, 589, 235, 584, 1265, 472).

It is a DELTA instrument, like `fz2_a1_rescore`: its absolute `bad` counts are
smaller than the banked ones because the window is shorter and they are **not**
comparable to the corpus bars.

## 5. THE EVIDENCE

### 5.1 The defect, on the rows

`intr_pending` is set in block (a) on the clock IE rises if the pin was up one
clock earlier, and is cleared **only by an interrupt entry**
(`v30u_eu_step.svh:838`).  Worked exemplar `fz2c/405072`: the pin falls at clock
629, IE rises at 629, the latch is set at 630, and at 632 `irq_int_lvl` is high
**solely** because of it (`int_p[2]` = the pin at 629 = 0).  The core enters at
636.  Silicon does not.

On **six of the seven** the latch-carried window opens at exactly `pin fall + 3`
— `int_p`'s own depth.  On the seventh (`fz2e/516038`) it opens **282 clocks**
after the fall, because IE was down in between: that is the demonstration that
the lifetime was unbounded and not merely three clocks too long.
