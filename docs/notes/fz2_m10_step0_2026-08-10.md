# M10 STEP ZERO — RUN, AND IT COMES BACK NAMED (2026-08-10)

**SIMPLICITY: this is 80's era hardware — nothing on the die is wasted. Complex
or confusing observed behavior is likely simple systems interacting in ways not
yet understood. A large fitted table, a many-cased rule, or a per-opcode special
case is a signal of misunderstanding, not a deliverable.**

Wave-4 diagnostic package, job 1.  **Offline, `sw/` + `docs/` only** — no RTL,
no board, no flash, no Quartus.  Tree `fuzz-v2-on-relanding` at `32128b57b4`.
This executes `docs/notes/fz2_m10_diagnosis_2026-08-10.md` **§6.0**, the free

## 0. THE HEADLINE, IN FOUR SENTENCES

1. **READING (i) IS ESTABLISHED AND M10 IS NOT AN EA FAMILY.**  On all six
   EMPTY seats the chip's registers at the fork already differ from the ucore's,
   the differing register is the one the forking ModR/M consumes, and it was
   written by an identifiable earlier instruction.
2. **That instruction is the same one on all six: `8D /r` with `mod == 3` —
   `LEA reg, reg`, the illegal register-operand form.**  Six of six carry one
   before the fork; on five the LEA's destination IS a base or index register of
   the forking EA, and on the sixth (`fz2e/522019`) a single retired
   `96 xchg si,ax` carries it there.
3. **This is not a new mechanism — it is a KNOWN, SOCKET-CHARACTERISED,
   ACCEPTED residue of this repo, reaching the address pins for the first
   time.**  `tests/v30/mod3_illegal` is its 128-golden socket characterisation
   (task #30, 2026-07-27); `sw/ucsim_check.py --residue stale-ea` is the gate
   that ACCEPTS the model's divergence, and its acceptance condition is that the
   divergence *stay confined to that one destination register*.  M10's residual
   is exactly the population where the confinement breaks.
4. **The directed 90-capture cell of §6.1 is NOT NEEDED and should not be
   spent.**  §6.0 said "if the same δ is what an earlier instruction's result
   would explain, reading (i) is established and the seats belong to whichever
   package owns that opcode."  It is, and they do.

## 2. THE SOLVE — one base, one δ, no other freedom

For each seat, with the ucore's own registers read out of the save-state map at
the fork (freeze 0):

* decode the forking group's ModR/M with the 16-bit addressing table verbatim
  (`rm → {BX+SI, BX+DI, BP+SI, BP+DI, SI, DI, BP/disp16, BX}`, `mod` →
  displacement length, `BP` ⇒ `SS` unless a retired override prefix says
  otherwise);
* check that `seg:(Σ regs + disp)` reproduces the **CORE's** address exactly —
  this is the gate, and it fails loudly rather than fitting;
* solve `chip = seg:(Σ regs + disp + δ)` for the single δ §6.0 authorises;
* report every `8D` mod3 retired at or before the fork with its distance in
  retired instructions and its destination register.

```
seat          dispatch      EA                    core EA ok    delta  8D-mod3 (dest, dist)
fz2c/406006   0f 3b 2a 8c   -                     None                 none
fz2c/406054   ff b8 2b 1a   DS:BW+IX+0x1a2b       True         -15133  8d dc -> BW (-3)
fz2c/408019   29 96 23 ca   SS:BP+0xca23          True           6044  8d eb -> BP (-21)
fz2e/518038   c1 62 06 9a   SS:BP+IX+0x0006       True           3607  8d ee -> BP (-6)
fz2e/522019   11 6c 9c      DS:IX+0xff9c          True          11008  8d c7 -> AW (-7)
fz2e/524034   84 82 84 65   SS:BP+IX+0x6584       True         -21783  8d f4 -> IX (-4)
fz2e/530001   86 79 68      DS:BW+IY+0x0068       True         -30703  8d d8 -> BW (-7)
fz2e/535027   a4            -                     none                 none
```

**The core-EA gate passes 6 / 6** on the EMPTY seats: the ucore computes the
architecturally-correct effective address to the bit, which is what M10 §5.4
already said and is here re-derived from the ModR/M byte rather than from the
solve's expression space.

**The two seats M10 did NOT call EMPTY carry no `8D` mod3 at all** — `406006`
(the pure segment fork on the undocumented `0F 3B`) and `535027` (the incoherent
`REPNZ MOVSB` seat).  Both are also the two whose forking instruction has no
decodable ModR/M EA, so they are reported `EA-NOT-DECODED` rather than scored.
**They are a 2 / 2 negative control inside the same family.**

## 3. THE TRANSFER PATH — every intervening instruction, listed

The retired stream from the LEA to the fork, `fz2_failview`'s reconstruction,
nothing elided:

| seat | LEA | between the LEA and the fork | writes the carrier? |
|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2c/406054` | `8d dc` **LEA BX, SP** | `mov dl,0x8c` · `jmp 0x77c6:0xc346` | **no** |
| `fz2c/408019` | `8d eb` **LEA BP, BX** | 20 instructions, of which **`33 ef xor bp,di`** | **yes — once, and XOR preserves the bit-difference** |
| `fz2e/518038` | `8d ee` **LEA BP, SI** | `and [si-0x59],al` · `aam` · `pop es` · `mov ax,[0xe8]` · `push 0xa919` | **no** |
| `fz2e/522019` | `8d c7` **LEA AX, DI** | `add bx,[bp+di]` · `push bp` · `aas` · **`96 xchg si,ax`** · `push es` · `pop dx` | **the transfer** — AX → SI, one hop |
| `fz2e/524034` | `8d f4` **LEA SI, SP** | `pop cx` · `pushf` · `cmp al,0xe8` | **no** |
| `fz2e/530001` | `8d d8` **LEA BX, AX** | `mov sp,0x4ceb` · `jmp` · `daa` · `push 0xffda` · `cmp al,0xa4` · `dec bp` | **no** |

Four of six: the LEA writes the register and nothing touches it again before the
fork.  One (`522019`): one `xchg si,ax`.  One (`408019`): one `xor bp,di`, which
is bit-preserving — both legs XOR with the same `DI` (they are bus-identical up
to the fork), so the bit-set the LEA disagreed on survives unchanged, and it is
`0x28A4`.

### 3.1 The value the chip put there

Core value read out of the save-state map at the fork; chip value = core + δ,
which is forced once the EA arithmetic above is exact:

| seat | carrier | core (ucore) | chip | δ | XOR |
|---|---|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `fz2c/406054` | `BW` | `0x122D` | `0xD710` | −15,133 | `0xC53D` |
| `fz2c/408019` | `BP` | `0xDC97` | `0xF433` | +6,044 | `0x28A4` |
| `fz2e/518038` | `BP` | `0x7262` | `0x8079` | +3,607 | `0xF21B` |
| `fz2e/522019` | `IX` | `0xD007` | `0xFB07` | +11,008 | `0x2B00` |
| `fz2e/524034` | `IX` | `0xD2B0` | `0x7D99` | −21,783 | `0xAF29` |
| `fz2e/530001` | `BW` | `0xD783` | `0x5F94` | −30,703 | `0x8817` |

**The chip's value is not the source register on any of the six** (`SP 0xA5FA`,
`BW 0x1979`, `IX 0xE49C`, `IY 0x0EDB`, `SP 0x2090`, `AW 0x6B35`), so `8D` mod3
is **not** a register-to-register move.  It is not any of the 21 named terms at
the fork either — which is expected and is not evidence, because the value was
latched several instructions earlier and the freeze sweep does not reach back
that far (§6.1).

### 3.2 THE END-OF-RUN ARCHITECTURAL DUMP CORROBORATES IT, EXACTLY, ON BOTH SEATS THAT HAVE ONE

Three of the six have both an `arch_words` (chip) and an `arch_sim_words` (core)
dump; on two of those three the carrier is not written again after the fork, and
on **both** the banked end-of-run dumps reproduce the predicted value to the
bit:

| seat | predicted chip = core + δ | measured chip | |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| `fz2e/518038` | 29,282 + 3,607 = **32,889** | **32,889** | **MATCH** — and `BP` is the *only* differing register in the whole 15-word dump |
| `fz2c/406054` | 4,653 − 15,133 = **55,056** | **55,056** | **MATCH** |

The third (`fz2c/408019`) is **not** expected to match and does not: its `BP`
went through `xor bp,di` and its two legs then diverged for 1,087 rows including
an `int 0x94`, so the additive δ has no reason to survive.  It is reported as a
non-match rather than dropped.

This is an **independent** confirmation: the end-of-run dump is read out of the
part through the terminator's store, on a completely different path from the
fork-row bus address the δ was solved from.

## 4. THE CONTROLS — the predicate's background rate

`python3 sw/fz2_m10_step0.py control`, same reconstruction, same rule, on the
whole 116-failure ledger:

| control | measurement |
|---|---|
| **A** — 200 random rows inside each of the 116 failures' own scored windows | an `8D` mod3 retired before the row: **2,131 / 23,200 = 9.19 %**.  Its destination IS a base/index register of the EA the instruction in dispatch at that row forms: **28 / 23,200 = 0.12 %** |
| **B** — the other **108** ledger failures at THEIR own fork rows | `8D` mod3 before the fork: **6 / 108 = 5.56 %**.  Destination is an EA register of the forking instruction: **0 / 108 = 0.00 %** |
| **the six M10 EMPTY seats** | `8D` mod3: **6 / 6**.  Destination directly an EA register: **5 / 6** (the sixth via one `xchg`) |

Binomial, one-sided, against control A's conjunctive rate `p = 0.001207`:

```
P(>= 5 of 6) = 1.5e-14        P(>= 6 of 6) = 3.1e-18
```

and against the bare `8D`-mod3 rate `p = 0.0919`, `P(6 of 6) = 6.0e-07`.
Against control B's `p = 0.0556`, `P(6 of 6) = 2.9e-08`.

**Read control A the right way round.**  `8D` mod3 is *common* in this corpus —
a raw-tier image is 64 K of random bytes and 9 % of random rows have one
upstream.  What is not common is its destination being consumed as an address
before the run ends: 0.12 %, and **zero times in 108 other failures**.  That
conjunction is the whole finding, and control A is conservative in the same way
M10 §3.2's was (random *rows* weight an instruction by its duration).

## 5. WHY THIS IS NOT A NEW MECHANISM — IT IS A REGISTERED ACCEPTED RESIDUE

`tests/v30/mod3_illegal/metadata.json`, task #30, `truth_source: SOCKET
(use_core=False, EMIT_USE_CORE)`:

> *"LEA reg,rm (mod3) loads the EU stale effective-address latch (last computed
> EA), independent of rm; 5 cycle-rows; falls through."*

`sw/ucsim_check.py::stale_ea_confined`, the gate that lets the model past it:

> *"On silicon that latch holds the last EA the pre-window injection stub
> computed; a from-scratch model has no such history, so the destination
> register cannot transfer.  **The gate is CONFINEMENT: the divergence must be
> exactly that one register and nothing else.**"*

All 128 goldens confirm the socket behaviour and its independence of `rm`: the
destination register comes back **1,280 = `0x500` = the harness's own `IP`** in
**128 of 128**, across all 8 destinations × all 8 sources × both `s` variants,
while the eight possible source registers hold eight distinct values
(`0x1111 0x2222 0x3333 0x4444 0x3F00 0x6666 0x7777 0x8888`).  Independence of
`rm` is therefore measured, not assumed.

`sw/testdata/fuzz_accept_rules.json` already carries the class:

> *"`lea_mod3` … Accepts iff the seed carries a lea-mod3 AND the arch-dump diff
> is confined to that LEA's dest register — strict fail-safe (anything else
> differing → refuse → surface)."*

**M10's residual six ARE the "anything else differing → surface" branch.**  The
divergence stopped being confined the moment the polluted register was used to
form an address, and it surfaced — on the bus, at the fork, as an "EA fork".
One of the six (`fz2e/530001`) is even banked with `sub = "lea-mod3"` and the
`lea_mod3` rule hit; the other five were labelled `open_bus` by an earlier rule
in the order, which is why the class was invisible from the verdict column.
Only **4 seeds in 3,840** carry the `lea-mod3` label at all, so the label was
never going to find this population.

**The metadata's own probe table already says the "last computed EA" reading is
not the whole law**: 2 of its 4 socket probes match (`MOV[1234h],AW` → `0x1234`,
`MOV[BEEFh],AW` → `0xBEEF`) and 2 do not (`MOV[3010h],AH` → `0x4121`,
`MOV CW,[4455h]` → `0xD4E5`).  That is stated here as the standing open half of
the mechanism, not as something this diagnostic found.

## 6. WHAT THE NEXT WAVE SHOULD DO

### 6.0 LEDGER HYGIENE — the label is wrong and the seats belong elsewhere

Carrying forward M10 §6.2 and adding to it:

1. **Move the six from `E1` to the `8D`-mod3 / stale-EA-latch package**, not to
   an EA package.  They are: `fz2c/406054`, `fz2c/408019`, `fz2e/518038`,
   `fz2e/522019`, `fz2e/524034`, `fz2e/530001`.
2. **`E1`'s remaining M10 content is TWO seats** — `fz2c/406006` (a genuine
   one-term segment fork on the undocumented `0F 3B`, `SS` where the core takes
   `DS`, same offset `0x970F`) and `fz2e/535027` (incoherent, re-triage) — plus
   M10 §6.2's 31 P4′ moves and 2 trap-delivery moves.
3. **The family label should not say "EA".**  Nothing about EA formation is
   wrong in any of the six; the ucore's ModR/M arithmetic is exact on 6 / 6.

### 6.1 THE NEXT FREE OFFLINE STEP — measure the latch, do not spend a capture

The remaining unknown is one thing only: **what the ucore writes on `8D` mod3
and what silicon writes, at the LEA's own clock.**  Both are readable offline
and neither needs a board.

* **The ucore's**: re-run `fz2_m10.py solve`'s save-state mode-6 freeze at the
  LEA's retirement row instead of the fork's — `fz2_m10_step0.json` carries each
  LEA's `f_row`.  Read `EA_RESIDUE` / `M_EA` / `R_EA` / `WB_EA` / `IND` /
  `TMPA` there and see which rail the ucore's destination equals.  This is the
  same instrument M10 already validated (offset measured to 0), pointed 3–21
  instructions earlier.
* **Silicon's**: the chip's value at the LEA is already derived here, in §3.1,
  to the bit, on six seeds with six different histories.  **Six data points on
  the V30's undocumented `8D` mod3 result, from the fuzz corpus, for free.**
  Compare them against each seed's own last-computed EA (reconstructable from
  the same retired stream, since both legs are bus-identical up to the fork) and
  the "last computed EA" reading is either confirmed or refuted on a population
  disjoint from the 128 goldens that produced it.

**Registered in advance, before that comparison is run**: the reading under test
is *"the destination takes the last effective address the EU computed before the
LEA"*.  If it holds on ≥ 4 of the 6 it is a mechanism; if it holds on ≤ 1 it is
refuted and the latch is something else.  Naming the bar now is the point.

### 6.2 §6.1's DIRECTED CELL IS WITHDRAWN

M10 §6.1 registered a 90-capture directed cell — `29 /r` with an explicit
register preamble, 8 `r/m` bases × 5 segment cases × 2 wait levels + 10 controls
— to be run **"if step zero comes back empty."**  It did not come back empty.
Its pre-registered bar was *"if all 80 cells match the architectural EA then
reading (i) is established by elimination"*; reading (i) is now established
**directly**, on six seeds, with an end-of-run corroboration on two of them and
a 0 / 108 negative control.  **Spending 90 socket captures to establish by
elimination something already established by measurement would be waste**, and
the cell's own registered logic says so.

**What a board cell WOULD still be worth**, if one is ever spent here: not
`29 /r` and not the EA at all, but **`8D` mod3 with a controlled pre-instruction
EA** — the `stale_ea_probe` table in `mod3_illegal/metadata.json` has four rows
and two of them already disagree with the stated law.  That is a four-row table
asking to be a sweep, and it is a *modelling* question, not an M10 question.

## 7. FALSIFIERS

* **Reading (i)** is refuted if, on any of the six, the carrier register is
  shown to be equal on both legs at the fork — e.g. by a `tb_sys` replay that
  reads the chip's register file there.  It is also refuted if the two exact
  end-of-run matches (§3.2) are shown to be arithmetic coincidences; at 1 in
  2¹⁶ each, two of two is 2⁻³².
* **The `8D` mod3 attribution** is refuted if the LEA is shown not to be the
  last writer of the carrier — every intervening instruction is listed in §3 and
  anyone can re-decode them — or if `fz2_m10_step0.py control` re-run with a
  different `--seed` puts the conjunctive background rate anywhere near 5 / 6.
  It is **also** refuted if an RTL landing that changes only the ucore's `8D`
  mod3 destination moves none of the six.
* **"M10 is not an EA family"** is refuted the moment any of the six is shown to
  have a wrong ModR/M sum; the sums are printed in §2 and are checked against
  the core's own bus address, 6 / 6.
* **The negative control** is refuted if `406006` or `535027` turns out to have
  an `8D` mod3 the reconstruction missed.

## 8. WHAT WAS NOT DONE, AND WHY

* **No board, no flash, no Quartus, no RTL.**  Everything here is banked or
  re-derived from banked captures.
* **No new instrument.**  The reconstruction is `fz2_failview`'s, the register
  dumps are M10's own mode-6 sweep, the ModR/M table is the ISA's, and the
  `8D` mod3 predicate is `ucsim_check.stale_ea_confined`'s.
* **The ucore's `8D` mod3 RTL was not read and not diagnosed.**  §6.1 says how
  to measure it; guessing at it from six δ values would be exactly the fitted
  table the standing principle forbids.
* **The three NOREPRO P4′ seats** (M10 §4.2) were not recovered; they are not
  in this residual and remain a `tb_sys` job for whoever wants them.
