## 3. **THE SEVEN NON-CLOSERS AND EIGHT OF THE NINE NON-CARRIERS ARE THE SAME COORDINATE, AND IT IS MEASURED, NOT ARGUED**

Anchored on the chip's own rows — the boundary at which the missed
recognition lands, and what precedes it:

| seat class | n | three ASCENDING `MEMR` pops (an `IRET`) immediately before, with **exactly two** code fetches between them and the boundary |
|---|---|---|
| carriers that CLOSED | 12 | **0 of 12** |
| carriers UNMOVED | 7 | **7 of 7** |
| non-carriers (P-3) | 9 | **8 of 9** |

The one carrier that has the pops and closed anyway, `fz2e/522051`, has
**four** fetches, not two — an instruction ran between the `IRET` and the
boundary — and it is the control that makes the coordinate readable as *the
`IRET`'s own end boundary* rather than *an `IRET` somewhere nearby*.  The one
non-carrier that does not fit, `fz2e/518039`, is not a missed trap at all:
**both legs read a vector-1 there**, the core's read is at `0x00006` with no
`0x00004` before it, and §38.9's predicate (first halves only) admitted it on
a technicality.

**CANDIDATE MECHANISM, BOOKED AND NOT IMPLEMENTED.**  At the boundary ending
an `IRET` that restored `PSW.TF`, silicon already owes the trap; the ucore
samples its arm there (`brk_arm := psw[FBRK] && brk_p[BRK_FLOOR-1]`) and can
only take it at the *next* boundary.  A one-boundary-late trap is normally
invisible — the storm re-aligns — and becomes a corpus failure in exactly two
ways, which is exactly the two seat classes above: the extra instruction is a
`HLT` (8 of 9 non-carriers, the survey's `B2`), or an external recognition
lands inside the window (7 of 7 unmoved carriers).

*Falsifier*: a capture in which the chip does **not** take a vector-1 trap at
the boundary ending an `IRET` that restored `TF`.  **THIS IS NOT LANDED HERE**
— §86's `BRK_FLOOR` is calibrated to 0 row-diffs over 30 directed captures at
depth 4 and nothing else within four orders of magnitude, so the rise
coordinate of an `IRET`'s `PSW` restore is a directed-cell question and a
landing of its own, not a term to be added by inspection.
