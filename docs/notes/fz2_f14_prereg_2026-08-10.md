`fz2_c2_rescore`) re-run the **core leg in Verilator** against banked chip

captures (`fz2c` 509 + `fz2e` 216) contain **all 198 ledger failures and all 3

Two consequences, both load-bearing:

* **The other 3,112 matched seeds have no banked rows at all.**  No offline

  covers **527 of the 3,639** currently-matched seeds (14.5 %).  **This
  re-capture is the first and only thing that can check the other 3,112**, and
  that is this sitting's registered exposure (P-7).

  match *and* its architectural dump matches.  Of the 46 seats predicted to
  close, **32 carry `arch = OK`** in the ledger — for those, rows are the only
  failing component and a rows-close *is* a ledger exit.  **14 carry a differing
  or absent dump** (C1's 12 and C2's 2) and need the arch to converge as well.
  That split is the sitting's principal registered uncertainty and it is
  registered as such in P-3 and P-4, **not discovered afterwards**.
