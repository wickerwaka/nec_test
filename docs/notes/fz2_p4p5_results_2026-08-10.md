## 1.  P4′ — **STOP AND BOOK, AS THE BRIEF'S OWN GUARD REQUIRES**

The law is confirmed and is one predicate (prereg §1.2).  What is measured is
that the ucore cannot supply its input in time:

| seat | opcode POP | ghost DISPLAY | `eu_bs` at the display | at the T1 | first `eu_bs == IOR` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2c/410028` | 2994 | **2994** | MEMW | MEMW | 2996 = pop+2 |
| `fz2e/528030` | 423 | **423** | MEMW | MEMW | 425 = pop+2 |
| `fz2e/527055` | 655 | **655** | MEMW | MEMW | 657 = pop+2 |
| `fz2e/520066` | 1246 | 1249 | **IOR** | **IOR** | 1248 = pop+2 |

Method: a temporary `+bstrace` block in `v30u_biu.sv` printing one line per CE
clock, replayed through `fz2_replay`'s own `tb_sys` path; the trace aligns to
the banked rows **300/300 on `ts`+`bs`** in each seat.  The block was removed
before the landing and is in no commit.

A live sample of `eu_bs` at the display, or at the T1, therefore closes exactly
**one** of the four — the seat where the bus stalled three extra clocks — and
the only pop+0 carrier in the BIU is `q_byte`, i.e. an opcode table.  **STOP
taken.**  Owner and falsifier are written into the pre-registration §1.4 and
into `v30u_biu.sv` beside the M7 comment is *not* required (nothing was changed
there); the booking lives in the two documents.

**The general finding underneath it.**  `QS` reports the queue operation of the
**previous** clock (8086/V30 pin definition) and this core publishes `qs` LIVE
at its own pop (`assign qs = … : pop_now ? …`).  For the `qs` column to match
silicon — and it does, 300/300 in every window above — the ucore's pop must sit
**one clock later on the pin timeline than silicon's**.  Every decode-derived
rail in this core is therefore one clock late *as seen from the bus*.  That is
invisible wherever the consumer is also a decode, and visible exactly where a
**bus announcement** meets a **decode** — which is this mechanism and, as far as
this sitting can tell, only this mechanism so far.

### 2.2  The four that did not — `flush_cs_we` is the row's INTENT

`wr_cs1` is asserted for the whole stall of the CS-writing row and `flush_cs`
during it is not the value that will be written (in `fz2e/533028` it is the
instruction's **displacement**, `7664`, against the `ccac` finally written).
The chip writes the register once and builds anything committed before that from
the value the register **holds**:

| seat | display | `we` level | `flush_cs` during it | actually written | chip's CS | ucore's |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2e/520062` | 700 | 691→702 | `2774` | `a243` | **`5cdb` = OLD** | `2674` |
| `fz2e/528008` | 628 | …→630 | `3b1f` | `3e05` | **`0ecc` = OLD** | `3a1f` |
| `fz2e/532012` | 328 | 320→330 | `bd85` | `64c3` | **`b674` = OLD** | `bc85` |
| `fz2e/533028` | 881 | 870→883 | `7664` | `ccac` | **`12fe` = OLD** | `7664` |

No flop-free BIU predicate separates "the register is written THIS clock" from
"a row that will write it is stalled"; the two candidates both fail (prereg
§2.2), and a flop would need a save-state address in single-writer territory.
**Owner: `v30u_eu.sv`.**  Booked in the RTL comment, with its own falsifier.
