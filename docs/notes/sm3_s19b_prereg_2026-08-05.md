### **F55 — A FINDING, BOOKED AND NOT LANDED.  F53's ADDRESS HALF IS NOT IN THE `ucore`; IT IS IN `tb_v30_core`'s COMPOSER.**

`ret` is **268, not 273**, and the 5-cell gap is exactly the 5 sweep cells §A
just closed in the model: `s13-hltsweep-w2 HLT.INT idx 10, 11` (first
divergence `(13, bus)`) and `-w3 idx 12, 13, 14` (`(15, bus)`).  On the S16
population the same mechanism costs **30** cells — `sm3_s16_fabric.py`'s
rows-only columns are `offline` **1,321 / 1,371** and `vsys_ret`
**1,291 / 1,371**, and the 30 disagreements are **exactly** the 30 family-E
cells, with the same coordinate on every one and **0 differing coordinates
anywhere else**.

The mechanism, read off `v30u_biu.sv` and confirmed on its own `+padtrace`:

> `halt_hold = r_run && r_cur_halt` holds `ad_oe_addr` asserted for the WHOLE
> HALT pseudo-cycle and publishes `r_cur_addr` on every clock of it.  F51's
> comment says *"the address it announced is never taken away from it and it
> stands on the pads for the whole pseudo-cycle"* — but silicon leaves it there
> by **RETENTION**, not by **DRIVE**, and the two differ exactly when a
> multi-clock announcement takes the pads in between and is then WITHDRAWN.
> `tb_v30_core.sv`'s `cycle_live` excludes a HALT-typed cycle from
> `core_ps_drive`, so the DEFAULT TB floats those clocks and scores the cells
> green **on the instrument's authority, not the core's**.  In `system_large`
> — and in fabric — the retention model keys on `AD_OE`, the core drives, and
> the withdrawn announcement's address is repainted.

**This is `standing_gates.md`'s meta-finding #5 exactly one law later**: *a
comparator that substitutes a value is asserting a mechanism, and the mechanism
needs its own falsifier.*  F53's UBE half **is** in the RTL (`last_ube`) and is
correct on both instruments; its ADDRESS half never was.

**IT IS DELIBERATELY NOT LANDED THIS SITTING.**  The fix is one term
(`halt_hold` becomes a one-shot on the address phase, which is exactly the term
§A took in the model), but landing it would put an unregistered RTL change into
the milestone's bitstream and confound the fabric confirmation of F53 and F54.
**It is booked as F55 with its falsifier, and the fabric leg below is its
directed test.**

*F55's falsifier, registered here for whoever takes it*: gate `halt_hold`'s
contribution to `ad_oe_addr`/`ad_o` to the address phase, and `tb_sys ret` must
go **268 → 273** and S16 `vsys_ret` **1,291 → 1,321** while `tb_v30_core`'s
own columns (273 / 283 and 1,321 / 1,371) do **NOT MOVE AT ALL** — because on
that instrument the change is invisible by construction.  Any movement of the
`tb_v30_core` column means the term was cut wider than the law.
