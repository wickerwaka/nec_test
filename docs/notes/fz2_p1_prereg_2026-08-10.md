## 0. TWO ERRATA AGAINST THE WAVE BRIEF, STATED FIRST

The brief supplied a candidate and a seat count.  **The artifact contradicts
both, and the artifact wins.**

### 0.1 THE BRIEF'S CANDIDATE (`flush_nmi_young`) IS REFUTED ON THE ROWS

The brief said: *"All 15 seats can only be withdrawn from the direct-T1 point
by `flush_nmi_young`, and its `<= 4` is a fitted threshold."*

Measured at each seat's own withdrawal clock (probe TB, §4):

* `flush_direct = !flush_stage && !flush_nmi_young && !flush_int_live` is 0 on
  every carrier **because `flush_stage = 1`**, which is upstream of
  `flush_nmi_young` in the same AND.  Deleting or re-fitting the threshold
  cannot move `flush_direct` on a single one of them.
* `flush_nmi_young` is **not even constant across the family** — it is 1 on
  seven carriers and 0 on five, while the divergence signature is identical on
  all twelve.  A term that varies while the outcome does not is not the
  carrier.
* And the direct-T1 point is not what silicon takes here.  Silicon takes an
  **ordinary eval on the flush clock** (announcement at `flush+1`, T1 at
  `flush+2`); `flush_fast`/`flush_direct` would open T1 **on the flush clock
  itself**, one clock EARLIER than silicon.

`flush_nmi_young` is untouched by this landing.

## 2. THE MECHANISM, FROM THE RTL

The withdrawal clock is an idle clock, and an idle clock is normally an eval
point (`v30u_biu.sv`, step (c)):

    end else if (!cmt_valid && !ne_now &&
                 !(flush_rep && flush_pend && !flush_staged_eval) &&
                 !eu_post_hold) begin
        ev_here = 1'b1;                       // end of an idle clock
    end

with

    wire flush_staged_eval = flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_int_live &&
                             !r_wr_done_p && flush_idle;

On all twelve, at the withdrawal clock:

    flush_rep=1  flush_stage=1  flush_pend=1  flush_nmi=1  flush_int_live=0
    flush_src_live=1  flush_idle=1  run=0  cmt_valid=0  rq_n=0  eu_post=0

so `flush_staged_eval = 0` **solely because `flush_int_live = 0`**, the eval is
suppressed, and the redirect is committed one clock later by F3's flush-only
point instead — which is exactly the `+1`.

**THIS IS THE SECOND HALF OF A1.**  A1 (`483c1f1270`) found that the
REP-withdrawal decision was reading the MASKABLE PACKAGE PIN where silicon
reads the RECOGNITION, and fixed it **in the display only** —
`v30u_biu.sv` line 685's `qs_e_now` now reads `flush_src_live =
flush_int_live || flush_nmi`.  A1's results §1 says in terms why the other
consumer was left alone: *"`flush_staged_eval` is kept … it has a SECOND
consumer, the idle clock's arbitration point in step (c), which is not a
display at all.  Deleting it would have changed arbitration."*

**That consumer is this one, and changing arbitration is this work item.**
A1's own §2 named it in advance: *"a SECOND, downstream mechanism is what
remains: after a REP withdrawal that is not bus-limited, the ucore's restart —
the redirected fetch and everything behind it — is still one clock late.  That
is the next work item and it is NOT claimed here."*

## 3. THE LAW, AS ONE PREDICATE, WITH ITS FALSIFIER

> **The REP withdrawal is decided by ONE rail in BOTH of its places.  A staged
> withdrawal yields the flush clock's arbitration point to the redirect exactly
> when a recognition is standing at the withdrawal — `flush_int_live ||
> flush_nmi`, whichever unit raised it — and nothing else is outstanding.  The
> redirect then commits on the withdrawal clock itself and its CODE status is
> announced at `withdrawal + 1`.**

No threshold, no counter, no interrupt-source identity, no opcode.  It is the
sentence A1 proved for the display, applied to the arbiter that the display
describes.

**FALSIFIER (registered).**  A staged REP withdrawal on an idle clock with a
standing recognition (maskable pin high *or* NMI latch set) and no outstanding
EU request, where silicon still announces the post-flush CODE status at
`withdrawal + 2`.

**SECOND FALSIFIER, for the deleted term.**  A staged REP withdrawal meeting a
standing recognition on an idle clock **with a write completion on the
preceding clock** (`r_wr_done_p = 1`), where silicon announces at
`withdrawal + 2`.  `!r_wr_done_p` is the term this landing deletes; the corpus
holds two such clocks (`fz2c/408000`, `fz2e/526025`) and **silicon announces at
`+1` on both**, so the term is falsified 2/2 on the only population that
exercises it here.  If a maskable-silicon gate refutes the deletion, the
fallback in §5 restores it and the landing is reported as closing 10 of 12.

## 5. THE EDIT

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_biu.sv`, one wire, and nothing else in the RTL:

    -wire flush_staged_eval = flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_int_live &&
    -                         !r_wr_done_p && flush_idle;
    +wire flush_staged_eval = flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_src_live &&
    +                         flush_idle;

`flush_src_live` **already exists** (A1 added it) and this is its second
consumer.  Two terms leave the predicate: the maskable-pin rail is replaced by
the recognition rail, and the fitted one-clock lookback `!r_wr_done_p` is
DELETED.  **The expression shrinks.**

* **No flop is added and none is removed.**
* **`v30u_ss_pkg.sv` and the two ss `.svh` files are NOT touched** — they are
  single-writer this round and this landing does not need them.
* No EU output, no new port, no opcode named, no per-seed case, no table.
* ⚠ **DECLARED SIDE EFFECT**: line 547 is `r_wr_done_p`'s only functional
  consumer, so after this landing `wr_done_p` is driven and saved but read only
  by the save-state mux.  Its flop and its SSA address (`SSA_B_WR_DONE_P`,
  `9'h05A`) are deliberately LEFT IN PLACE — retiring an SSA address is the ss
  owner's call, not this landing's, and `ss_lint` must therefore be UNMOVED.
  Booked for the ss owner, not taken here.

**THE FALLBACK (`P1-B`), REGISTERED IN ADVANCE WITH ITS DECISION RULE.**  If
any gate carrying maskable-interrupt silicon (§6 P-3, P-4, P-5 — headline
`check_core --opcodes all`, whose `INT.F3AA` cell is the 35-withdrawal
population `flush_int_live` was measured on) comes back RED, the deletion of
`!r_wr_done_p` is **REFUTED and reported as refuted**, `P1-B` restores it
(`flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_src_live && !r_wr_done_p && flush_idle`),
and the landing is scored on the same gate set with a registered closure of
**10 of 12** (`fz2c/408000` and `fz2e/526025` stay open — they are the two
carriers with `r_wr_done_p = 1`).  **No third form will be tried in this
sitting.**
