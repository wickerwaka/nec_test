## 1. THE BRIEF IS WRONG ABOUT THIS FAMILY, AND THE ARTIFACT WINS

The task brief describes `A1` as *"the QS **pop** announced one row late … the
pop coincides with the last wait state of a write"*, and points at
`fz2c/407027` as *"a MEMW with waits"*.  **Three of those clauses do not
survive the rows.**

1. **The announcement is `QS = E`, which is the queue-EMPTY (flush)
   announcement, not a pop.**  `fuzz_classify.QS_NAME` is
   `{0:'-', 1:'F', 2:'E', 3:'S'}` and every one of the 23 first-divergence rows
   carries chip `qs = 2`.  The ledger's own signature says so: `qs E!=-` on
   23 of 23.  No byte is popped on these rows.
2. **It is not anchored to a write's last wait state.**  Over the 23, the chip's
   `E` row falls on `Tw` 5 times, on `T4` twice and on an idle `Ti` 14 times;
   where it falls on a `Tw` it is the *second* of three (`fz2e/521075`), the
   *second* of five (`fz2e/515042`) and the *third* of three (`fz2c/407027`).
   The bus phase is incidental.
3. **All 23 are INTERRUPT ENTRIES** — 22 of them the rig's own terminating NMI
   (the `E` sits 16-29 rows after the NMI pin asserts and is followed by the
   vector-2 read at linear `0x00008` within 9-15 rows on all 22), and the
   23rd (`fz2e/515042`) an INTA-vectored entry from the seed's stimulus event.

What **is** exactly as the brief says: the offset is `+1` on **23 of 23**, the
pre-divergence cycle sequence is identical on all of them, and the mechanism is
register-side and needs no new state.

**A second correction, smaller:** the family is not uniformly a two-row blip.
On **15 of 23** the whole entry moves with the announcement — the vector read,
the pushes and the restart fetch are all `+1` — and on **8** the slip is
absorbed because a bus cycle was already running.  That is one mechanism, not
two: the `+1` shows on the pins immediately, and downstream only where the bus
had no work of its own to hide it.

## 2. THE MECHANISM, MEASURED INSIDE THE ucore

Instrument: a throwaway Verilator build of the ucore + `tb_v30_core` in a
scratch tree (repo untouched), with one `$fdisplay` of BIU/EU rails on the row
emit, so an internal rail can be read against the same row index the capture
uses.

At the CHIP's `E` row, **20 of the 23** carry exactly one state:

    q_flush = 1   flush_rep = 1   flush_stage = 1   flush_pend = 1
    flush_nmi = 1  flush_int_live = 0  flush_direct = 0
    qs_port_fetch = 0   absorb_ttl = 0   pop_now = 0   e_from_block = 0

That is: **the ucore's flush is on the SAME clock as silicon's `E`.**  Nothing
is late except the DISPLAY, and the display is held by exactly one factor of
`qs_e_now` (`v30u_biu.sv`) — the REP-withdrawal suppression's STAGED arm:

    !(q_flush && flush_rep &&
      (flush_direct ||
       (flush_pend && !(r_run && !r_cur_fetch && (r_ts < TS_T3))
                   && !flush_staged_eval)))

`flush_staged_eval` is the arm's only escape and it reads
**`flush_int_live` — the MASKABLE package pin — and nothing else**:

    flush_staged_eval = flush_stage && flush_pend && flush_int_live
                        && !r_wr_done_p && flush_idle

On these 20 the recognition standing at the withdrawal is the **non-maskable**
one (`flush_nmi`, the EU's `nmi_latch`), so the escape cannot fire and `E` is
carried to the next clock by `e_pend`.

**The block's own comment already states the law this is a hole in**
(`v30u_biu.sv`, the paragraph beginning *"THE ASSERTED PIN WITHDRAWS THE DIRECT
POINT ON *EITHER* TAIL"*): *"It is ONE rail and it acts the same way on both:
while the … pin is still asserted at the withdrawal the redirect takes an
ordinary idle eval, so EMPTY stands on the FLUSH row."*  Both of the
populations that sentence was measured on are **maskable-pin** populations.
The non-maskable rail was never given to the staged arm.

The remaining **3 of 23** (`fz2e/509036`, `fz2e/513055`, `fz2e/515042`) are
`stim`-stratum seeds whose flush source is the seed's OWN interrupt event.  The
offline instrument (§4) has ONE event scheduler and must spend it on the
terminating NMI, so their entries do not occur in the replay at all.  **They are
declared OUT OF SCOPE of this sitting's measurement and are NOT counted as
closed.**

## 3. THE LAW, AS ONE PREDICATE, WITH ITS FALSIFIER

> **A REP withdrawal whose recognition is still standing at the withdrawal
> shows EMPTY on the FLUSH ROW.  The rail is the recognition, not the maskable
> pin: `flush_int_live || flush_nmi`.**

Everything else about the display is unchanged, and in particular the queue
port still wins: a fetch that owns the port (`qs_port_fetch`) still holds `E`
off, and **silicon agrees** — `fz2c/408062` is a banked `SUCCESS` in which the
predicate's own rails are all set at row 3257 while a CODE fetch is in `Tw`,
and BOTH legs put `E` at 3259.  That seed is the control that says this law is
about the staged arm and not about the port.

**FALSIFIER (registered).**  A capture in which a staged REP withdrawal meets a
standing recognition — maskable pin high or NMI latch set — with the queue port
otherwise free, and silicon still delays `EMPTY` to the following clock.

**THE EDIT (primary, `L1`).**  `hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_biu.sv` only:

* `flush_staged_eval` (5 terms) is **DELETED** — it is subsumed, because it
  required `flush_int_live`, which the new term already excludes;
* one wire is added: `flush_src_live = flush_int_live || flush_nmi`, off two
  rails the EU already publishes;
* the staged arm gains `&& !flush_src_live`.

Net: the expression SHRINKS.  **No flop, no save-state address, no new EU
output, no opcode named, no per-seed case.**

**THE FALLBACK (`L2`), REGISTERED IN ADVANCE WITH ITS DECISION RULE.**  `L1`
also changes the MASKABLE staged case that has `r_wr_done_p` set or
`flush_idle` clear — currently suppressed, under `L1` not.  If any gate in §6
that carries maskable-interrupt silicon (headline: `check_core --opcodes all`,
whose `INT.F3AA` cell is the 35-withdrawal population `flush_int_live` was
measured on) comes back RED under `L1`, `L1` is **REFUTED and reported as
refuted**, and `L2` — the same term restricted to the rail that has no cell
anywhere in the tree, `&& !flush_nmi`, with `flush_staged_eval` kept — is
taken as an erratum and scored on the same gate set.  **No third form will be
tried in this sitting.**

## 4. THE OFFLINE RESCORE INSTRUMENT, DECLARED BEFORE IT IS USED

`sw/fz2_a1_rescore.py`.  For each of the **725 RETAINED fz2 captures**:

* the seed's image is regenerated through the campaign's own generator with the
  seed's own stratum overrides (`fz2_w1.ov_of`), and its **sha256 is ASSERTED
  against the banked result line** — a mismatch is `GEN_DRIFT` and is reported,
  never scored;
* it is replayed on **the TREE's receipted `--core ucore` `tb_v30_core`
  binary** (`check_seq.run_tb(..., core="ucore")`; the module default is pinned
  to the ARCHIVED fsm core and is explicitly overridden — `standing_gates.md`
  F-4), with the campaign's own wait source (`wvec` / `wrand` / fixed) and with
  **the campaign's own terminating NMI armed** on the TB's single scheduler
  (`fuzz_campaign.term_directive`);
* the rows are compared against the **BANKED SOCKET (chip) rows** with
  `fuzz_classify.diff_rows`.

**THE WINDOW IS A RULE FIXED BEFORE ANY RESULT IS SEEN.**  The TB has one
scheduler and no `TVEC` vector-substitution overlay, so it is a faithful replay
only up to the earlier of (a) the chip's first read of the SUBSTITUTED vector
(a `MEMR` T1 at linear `0x00008` / `0x0000A`) and (b) the assertion row of any
stimulus pin event the TB could not arm beside the terminator.  The window is
`min(banked win, (a), (b))`.  Past it the two legs execute different code BY
CONSTRUCTION and nothing there is scored.

⚠ **THIS IS NOT A SILICON-MATCH RATE AND MAY NOT BE QUOTED AS ONE.**  It is a
DELTA instrument: the identical instrument runs before and after, and the only
readable quantity is WHICH SEEDS MOVED.  Its absolute pass count is depressed
by the instrument's own two limits and says nothing about the part.

**Why not `tb_sys`**: `tb_sys` carries the schedulers and `TVEC` but has NO
`wrand`/`wvec` leg (`x1_retention.vsys_run` refuses both), so it cannot
reproduce the wait pattern of most of this corpus — and `tb_sys` is the
concurrent agent's territory this sitting.

## E-1. ERRATUM TO §4's WINDOW RULE — WRITTEN AND COMMITTED BEFORE THE RTL EDIT, AND THE BASELINE IS RE-MEASURED UNDER IT

> **THE TIMING.**  The FIRST baseline was run at `709bf3ba9e` with the tree
> clean of RTL changes (`CLEAN 630 / 725`, 0 errors, 0 `GEN_DRIFT`, DUT receipt
> `fd530fae48239a7e…`).  This erratum is written from **that baseline alone**;
> **no post-change number exists**, the RTL has not been touched, and the
> corrected instrument is re-run on the SAME unchanged tree before anything
> else happens.

§4's cut had two defects, both visible in the before-leg and both making the
window too SHORT — i.e. both hiding the family this sitting is trying to score.

**(a) The vector-read cut fired on ordinary data.**  A raw seed is 64 K of
random bytes and legitimately reads linear `0x00008` as DATA long before the
terminator exists.  `fz2e/518065`'s window was cut at row **1,593** by such a
read, while its scored divergence is at 3,317.  **CORRECTED**: the cut is the
first such read **at or after the terminating NMI's own assertion row**, which
is the row the banked line already carries.

**(b) The stimulus-event cut was over-broad, and removing it is CONSERVATIVE.**
`fz2c/409066`'s window was cut at row **156** — its stimulus pin's assertion —
while its A1 divergence is at 3,321 and the replay is exact to that row.  The
TB has one scheduler and must spend it on the terminator, so a seed whose
stimulus actually matters simply DIVERGES; that divergence is identical in the
before and after legs and can therefore only ever **hide a gain, never
manufacture one**.  **CORRECTED**: the stimulus cut is REMOVED.

**Nothing else about the instrument moves**, both legs are measured under the
corrected rule, and §5's seat list is re-registered below against the corrected
BEFORE leg — a before-leg quantity, measured on the unchanged tree.
