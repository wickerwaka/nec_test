### A.1 THE DIAGNOSIS — IT IS THE WITHDRAWN ANNOUNCEMENT, AND IT IS ALL THREE PINS, NOT ONE

Read off `s13-hltsweep-w2 HLT.INT idx 10` (golden `G`, model `M`), rows 8-14:

| row | | bus | ube | data | busstat | T |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | G=M | `057CB4` | 1 | `7CB4` | CODE | T3 |
| 10 | G=M | `067CB4` | **0** | `7CB4` | CODE | Tw |
| 11 | G=M | `067CB4` | 0 | `7CB4` | CODE | Tw |
| 12 | **G** | **`067CB4`** | **0** | **`7CB4`** | PASV | T4 |
| 12 | **M** | `067CB2` | **1** | `7CB2` | PASV | T4 |
| 13 | **G** | `007CB4` | 0 | `7CB4` | INTA | Ti |
| 13 | **M** | `007CB2` | 1 | `7CB2` | INTA | Ti |

Rows 9-11 are the wake fetch's ANNOUNCEMENT — a multi-clock display window
because the HALT pseudo-cycle still owns the bus.  Row 9 is its display clock
(A19-16 = its own address nibble `5`), rows 10-11 are `data_ps(CS)` = `6`
(F53's address one-shot, which the model already has at M23), and UBE turns 0 on
row 10, one clock after the status (M2).

**Then the announcement is WITHDRAWN** — the acknowledge takes the register
instead — and on row 12, the HALT pseudo-cycle's own T4, silicon shows the
WITHDRAWN cycle's three pins still standing.  The model repaints all three with
the HALT's own: `067CB2`, `7CB2`, `ube 1`.  Row 13's `bus`/`data` follow because
an INTA freezes the FLOATING AD into its access on its display clock
(`biu_timed.cpp` 357, `cmt_.addr = last_data_`), so the model's wrong retention
propagates one cycle further.

**This is ucsim-t §26.7.7's open item and `ucore_provenance.md` §77.A's family E,
and §79.I's booking of it as "F53's UBE half" IS TOO NARROW: it is the same
one-shot's three pins, exactly as §77.A.1 states the law.**  The ucore carries
both halves already — the UBE half in the core (`v30u_biu.sv` `ube_n`'s
`last_ube` term, F53b) and the AD half in the pad model (`tb_v30_core.sv`'s
`cycle_live`, which excludes a HALT-typed cycle from `core_ps_drive`, and in
fabric the pads themselves).  **The model has no pad-drive layer — it emits the
COMPOSED row — so it must carry both, and they are one sentence.**

### A.2 THE LAW TO BE LANDED — TWO SENTENCES ALREADY WRITTEN DOWN, NEITHER OF THEM NEW

> **(i) F53b.**  UBE is loaded by the ADDRESS PHASE and then HELD.  It is not
> re-driven by a cycle that is merely running.
> **(ii) F51.**  A HALT pseudo-cycle has NO DATA PHASE.  After its address phase
> it drives nothing, and the pads hold whatever was last put on them.

No new constant, no per-delay table, no per-program case, no new state (the
`last_*` retention registers already exist and are already used by the idle
branch).  The two sentences are ONE mechanism — *a pad is loaded by a phase and
held otherwise* — and where no announcement intervenes, "hold" and "re-drive"
are the SAME VALUE by construction, which is why 168,997 w0 goldens cannot see
either.
