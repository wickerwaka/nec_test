### A-1.1a **THE SIGN ANOMALY WENT THE SAME WAY A THIRD TIME — REPORTED, NOT EXPLAINED**

Registered obligation, §1.1.  On this tree:

    CONTROL     40.00 MHz   +6.249 ns    (two identical draws, dc2f86b625)
    RETENTION   42.24 MHz   +7.574 ns    (two identical draws, this addendum)
    difference  +2.24 MHz, +1.325 ns  -- RETENTION ABOVE CONTROL

That is the **third consecutive inversion on this branch**: FLASH #13
39.37 → 39.83, FLASH #14 39.47 → 40.97, FLASH #15 40.00 → 42.24.  Before this
branch, retention drew at or below control every time it was measured (SM3 s19
45.49 → 44.99, s27 47.85 → 45.72, W4 47.31 → 46.74, this branch's own base
41.80 → 40.96).

**It is no longer a coincidence of one draw and it is still not explained.**
Three of three, each with two identical draws on each side, is six compiles;
the retention configuration adds a small observation-path register bank
(`hb_ad_sample`) inside `system_large` and nothing on the core's critical cone.
**Registered as a standing observation with a falsifier**: build CONTROL and
RETENTION from the same tree on three further landings; if the sign inverts
again the effect is real and belongs to the fit's seed distribution rather than
to the retention model, and the multi-seed worst-of-N gate `standing_gates.md`
§A still calls for is the only instrument that can say which.

**No Fmax claim is made and no band is registered** (§1.1 stands unchanged).
`standing_gates.md` §A governs: the same tree has drawn 19.42 and 45.91 MHz,
and two identical draws are not a distribution.
