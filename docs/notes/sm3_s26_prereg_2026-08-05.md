# A. `TAIL_EXTRA` — THE MECHANISM, NAMED BEFORE THE LANDING

## A.0 §86.F asked for one mechanism covering the population.  Here it is, and
## it is the `F` / OPR interlock with nothing to release it.

§86.F surveyed the family and named the *form set* — `62` CHKIND, `C4` LES,
`C5` LDS, `FE`/`FF` group `/3` and `/5`, all at `mod == 3` — plus the reading
that they are "forms whose microcode must read a multi-word memory operand".
That is a **description of a list**.  This pre-registration names the
**predicate that generates the list**, which is a different and much smaller
object:

> **`OPR` is the EU's read-data / write-data register, and the `F` bit on a
> micro-row is the interlock that waits for the bus access that fills it
> (`biu_timed.h`: "THE F INTERLOCK IS THE **OPR** INTERLOCK").  A micro-row
> that SOURCES `OPR` under an `F` therefore waits for a read.  When the
> decoder issued no operand pre-read — which is exactly what `mod == 3` means
> — and the microcode has issued none of its own yet, that row is waiting for
> an access that will never be posted.  THE EU PARKS.  It is not `HALT`: no
> `HLT` row ran, no `HALT` status is driven.  The prefetcher keeps running
> until the queue is full and then the bus sits `PASV`.**

That is one sentence about one register and one already-existing interlock,
and it reproduces all four columns of §86.F's invariant table without naming
an opcode:

| §86.F's measured invariant | what the predicate says |
|---|---|
| the chip's last bus cycle is a `CODE` fetch, 29/29 | the stall is in the EU; the BIU's last act is the prefetch that was already in flight |
| the engine's first EXTRA cycle is a `MEMR`, 29/29 | the very access the parked row was waiting for, which the engines wrongly go on to issue on the *next* row |
| neither side ever drove the `HALT` status, 0/29 | no `HLT` micro-row is involved |
| the chip then sits `PASV`, `qs = 0`, pads frozen, 957–3,906 rows | queue full, EU not popping, prefetcher idle |

## A.1 THE PREDICATE IS EXACT, AND THAT IS **MEASURED**, NOT ARGUED

The falsifier for "one mechanism covers the population" is a form that
satisfies the predicate and does NOT stall, or a stalling form the predicate
misses.  Both directions were swept **before** writing this document, on the
frozen `sim/build/v30sim` (receipt `8392b3fccb7b1c5b…`), by tracing the model's
own micro-row execution and flagging any row that sources `OPR` before any
read has loaded it in that instruction:

| sweep | forms probed | predicate fires on |
|---|---|---|
| native page, every opcode `00`–`FF` × every ModR/M `reg` 0–7, **`mod == 3`** | 2,048 | **`62` (all reg), `C4` (all reg), `C5` (all reg), `FE` reg∈{3,5}, `FF` reg∈{3,5}** |
| native page, the same, **`mod == 0` `rm == 6`** (memory, i.e. the pre-read runs, and for `MODRM_STORE` forms it does not) | 2,048 | **NOTHING** |
| `0F` extended page, every opcode × every `reg`, `mod == 3` | 2,048 | **NOTHING** |
| `0F` extended page, the same, memory | 2,048 | **NOTHING** |

**8,192 forms; the predicate fires on exactly §86.F's family and on nothing
else in the whole opcode space.**  `FE`/`FF` `/3` and `/5` — the two the
2026-07-27 `mod3_illegal` metadata does not name and the survey ADDED — fall
out of the predicate rather than being put in by hand, and they do so because
`FE` and `FF` share micro-page 3 (`loader_impl.h`'s group dispatch puts the
ModR/M byte in the opcode slot), which is why the byte form `FE /3` behaves
like the word form `FF /3`.  `FF /7` — an equally undefined group form, and
one that DOES appear at `mod == 3` in a golden suite — does **not** fire,
because its row `0FE0.2` is `M -> OPR`: it WRITES the register instead of
sourcing it.  `8D` LEA at `mod == 3` does not fire either, which is the
`stale-ea` residue the suite already carries and is why the FSM core's old
`S_HALT` wedge on LEA was a BUG while its wedge on `62`/`C4`/`C5` was correct.

## A.2 THE PREDICATE IS CONFIRMED AT THE SEEDS' OWN STOP CLOCK

`V30SIM_ROWTRACE` on the banked seeds, scored against the chip's LAST bus-cycle
`T1` in the same capture.  Example, `t30-raw/72` (chip's last `T1` at row 680,
a `CODE` fetch of `0x00538`):

```
RT 680 01C8 OPR    -> tmpb    SIGMA  -> tmpa    F    ALU INC2   tmpa
RT 681 01C9 SIGMA  -> IND                            CTL SUSP   MEMR
RT 682 01CA OPR    -> CS                        F    CTL
RT 691 01CB tmpb   -> PC                          E  CTL FLUSH
```

Row 680 — **the chip's stop clock to the clock** — is `FF /5` JMP FAR's
`OPR -> tmpb … F`, and row 681 is the `MEMR` the census calls the first extra
cycle.  Over the 35 seeds (`sim` 30 REG + 3 EVT, `ucore` 29 REG + 5 EVT, union),
the micro-row the model is standing on within ±3 rows of the chip's stop is in
one of the five banks `0278`(62) `00F8`(C4) `00FC`(C5) `01C0`(FF/3) `01C8`(FF/5)
on **32 of 35**; the three that are not — `t30-brkem/185`, `t30-raw/496`,
`t30-raw/638` — are named here IN ADVANCE as the seeds most likely to miss.

# B. `mc1/721` — THE POST-`E` DISCHARGE ORDER

## B.0 THE DIAGNOSIS IS §86.G's AND IT IS NOT RE-OPENED

Both writes land; they land in the WRONG ORDER.  `9E` SAHF's flag write lives
on its POST-`E` row (`007E tmpa -> FLAGS`), and on the E-row PRE-POP path
(`row_epop`) the `ucore` raises `poste_n` INSIDE the chain, after block (b) —
which discharges `poste` at the TOP of the edge — has already run.  The
successor's zero-cost loader steps (`S_TAIL → S_INSTR_END → S_TAKE_OPC →
S_DECODE → S_DECODE2`) and its `ONE_BYTE_LOGIC` write ride the SAME edge, so
the successor's write commits first and the post-`E` slips to the next clock
and writes on top of it.  Measured: `1BL clk=303 pre=f202 post=f203` then
`PE clk=304 upc=9e2`.
