## §A.4 **WHAT FLASH #10 CAN AND CANNOT EXERCISE — DERIVED, NOT ASSUMED**

Two of the five landings are **not reachable by any fabric population this
sitting**, and that is established by reading the populations rather than by
argument:

* **The four HLT sweeps (283 cells) and the S16 walk (1,371 cells) are the SAME
  ONE-BYTE PROGRAM.**  Scanned over all 1,654 goldens: the byte image is
  `[0xF4]` — `HLT` — in **every** case, and `PSW.TF` is **clear in every one of
  the 1,654** initial states.  So:
  * **the BRK/TF single-step trap is NOT exercisable there** — it arms on
    `PSW.TF` and no case sets it;
  * **the illegal-form stall is NOT exercisable there** — it fires only on
    `62` / `C4` / `C5` / `FE`,`FF` `/3`,`/5` at `mod == 3` (§87.A's 8,192-form
    sweep) and none of those bytes is in the image.
* **What the sweeps and the S16 walk DO score** is display/HALT behaviour, which
  is precisely F55's, F56's and F57's surface.  **The whole predicted fabric
  delta over FLASH #9 is therefore F55 + F56 + F57 and nothing else**: sweeps
  268 → 279 (+5 F55, +6 F56/F57), S16 1,291 → 1,347 (+30 F55, +26 F56/F57).
* **The BRK/TF trap's silicon evidence is a SOCKET population and already
  banked.**  `sw/sm3_tf_floor_cell.py` scores the 30 retained captures in
  `sw/testdata/sm3-s24tfcell/`; the trap is internal, drives no pin, and the
  captures are deterministic from RESET, so **no board contact is needed for it
  and none is taken**.  Its ucore leg (121,860 rows, 0 row-diffs, depth 4 and no
  other depth in [1,7]) is a HEAD figure and is re-asserted in half B, not
  re-captured.
* **The trap and the stall reach fabric only through the b3 priority tranche**
  (P5), whose images are random 64 KiB soup and whose offline `vsim_ucore`
