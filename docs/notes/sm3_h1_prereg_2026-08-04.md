## §1 WHAT THE SOCKET SAYS, BEFORE ANY MODEL IS CONSULTED

Three chip-side measurements, all read off banked `chip_rows` with no engine in
the loop.  Tools: `sw/sm3_ackgeom.py`, `sw/sm3_popgeom.py` (measurement tools,
never gates).

### §1.1 The acknowledge's lead-in — 2,318 re-entry acknowledges, ZERO exceptions

Over the 1,008-seed EVT bank, every acknowledge that is **not** the first one of
its record (i.e. not the wake from HALT) is preceded on the bus by a **CODE
fetch** — 2,318 of 2,318 — and

```
    INTA1 T1  =  max( F1 + 6 ,  F1 + L + 1 )
```

with `F1` that fetch's T1 row and `L` its length in clocks.  Measured, by `L`:

| L (fetch length) | 4 | 5 | 6 | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12..19 |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| acknowledges | 1597 | 363 | 170 | 116 | 12 | 14 | 16 | 9 | 21 |
| `INTA1 T1 − F1` | **6** | **6** | 7 | 8 | 9 | 10 | 11 | 12 | L+1 |

`F1 + L + 1` is the ORDINARY next-cycle slot (M2r: the winner's T1 is `e+2`, and
`e = L−2` at w0, `L−1` under waits).  So the second term is the bus and carries
no new number.  **The whole of H1 is the first term, and it bites at `L = 4`
alone, where it costs exactly 2 clocks.**  It is the fetch's OWN length that
selects the row, not the seed's wait class: inside `wrand1` seeds the `L = 4`
acknowledges are at gap 6 and the `L = 5` ones at gap 6 as well.

### §1.2 The FIRST acknowledge does not pay it

Same census, the wake from HALT: with `L = 4` the acknowledge opens at
`F1 + 4` — the back-to-back slot, no floor (295 of 306 golden-shaped cells, and
the census's own 443/445).  Whatever the floor is, it is a property of a
**boundary recognition**, not of the INTA cycle.

### §1.3 M2r's `e+3` is confirmed on the socket, and it is NOT the floor

`sw/sm3_popgeom.py`, 50,422 flush→pop events over all 3,242 banked seeds: the
first opcode pop after a queue flush lands at the refilling fetch's

```
    e + 3   =  F1 + 5 / 7 / 8 / 9   at L = 4 / 5 / 6 / 7      (25,670 / 10,222 / 5,496 / 3,918)
```

which is §11.1 M2r's *"a fetched byte becomes POPPABLE at e+3"*, re-derived
from silicon.  The floor is at `F1 + 6`, i.e. **the acknowledge opens BEFORE the
byte is poppable at every wait level ≥ 1** — so the floor is not "wait for the
byte", and S9a's *"the recognition boundary is the retire, not the pop"* stands.

## §3 THE CANDIDATE READINGS, AND WHAT KILLS EACH

| | reading | status |
|---|---|---|
| **C0** | *the entry sequence costs 2 more clocks: `entry = B + 4`, not M14's `B + 2`* | **REFUTED OFFLINE.**  The `INT.*` / `NMI.*` w0 goldens are **B-limited in 1,266 of 1,800 cases** (`A+3 < B`; measured by running the suites with `V30SIM_EVTTRACE`), and the model is 200/200 on every one of them with `B + 2`.  A uniform `+2` moves all of them. |
| **C2** | *an IE-restoring instruction defers the recognition by 2* | **REFUTED OFFLINE.**  `INT.9D` (POPF) and `INT.FB` (STI) are golden forms, B-limited 190/200 and 160/200, and exact with no deferral. |
| **C3** | *it is `IRET` specifically* | **NOT SEPARABLE OFFLINE** — every re-entry in the bank follows an IRET, and no golden form has a queue-flushing anchor instruction.  **This is what the board cell is for.**  Forbidden by the simplicity principle as a deliverable, but it must be excluded by measurement, not by taste. |
| **C1** | *the recognition boundary after a REDIRECT sits at the RESTARTED PREFETCH's index 2* | **LANDED in `sim/` on §1.1's 2,318 acknowledges** (§4).  Index 2 is not a new number: it is the cycle-relative instant this machine already samples the queue counter on (`pf_arm_`, MEASURED on the Arm-C sled), and at w0 it IS the completion eval. |
| **C1′** | *…at the REDIRECT's own commit + 4* | **NOT SEPARABLE OFFLINE.**  In the whole bank the restarted prefetch is granted at the redirect's `e+2` with no exception, so `restart T1 + 2 ≡ redirect commit + 4`.  **The board cell must delay the restart.** |

## §4 WHAT WAS LANDED IN `sim/` BEFORE THE BOARD CONTACT (C1)

`BiuTimed::bnd_floor_`: a flush ARMS it, the GRANT of the restarted prefetch
STAMPS it at that fetch's T1 + 2, a pop SPENDS it, and
`BiuTimed::boundary_no_pop()` — S9a's recognition edge, and **only** that —
reads it.  Plus, in `run_timed_boot`, the recognition SUSPENDS the prefetcher on
its own decision clock (the eval in between would otherwise hand the slot to a
prefetch the chip does not run).

**Two bounded claims, both stated because both were tried and MEASURED:**

1. the floor is **not** applied to the ordinary retire (`opcode_prefetch`).
   It is unobservable there — after a redirect the successor's pop is
   byte-limited at `e+3` = index 5 or later, strictly after index 2 — and
   charging the EU to it anyway costs `INT.F3AA` **75 of 200** at w0.
2. the floor is **not** applied to the REP mid-string withdrawal
   (`boundary_no_pop(post_redirect = false)` when `intr_pending`).  A
   withdrawal is not an instruction retire: its recognition was taken inside
   the loop (§19.8.1) and the ROM's REPX path flushes AFTER that decision.
   Flooring it too costs `INT.F3AA` **26 of 200** at w0 and nothing elsewhere.
   §26.6.4 already recorded that the sweep cells and the seven
   `bs PASV!=INTA` withdrawal seeds *"point OPPOSITE WAYS"*; this is that
   sentence, in code.

## §5 THE DIRECTED CELL — PRE-REGISTERED PREDICTIONS

**Board discipline**: single-writer checked, socket only (`use_core=False`,
explicit), `div_guard` readback recorded on every probe, full per-clock rows +
sha256 retained, `board_idle` at the end, a wedge STOPS the session.  **NO
FLASHING** — the board carries FLASH #4 and this cell does not need the core.

**The stimulus.**  A tight loop whose closing instruction is the ANCHOR, with
the INT level raised and HELD long enough to be recognised at the first boundary
after it arrives, swept over the assert delay so the recognition lands at every
phase, at waits 0/1/2/3.

| variant | anchor | what it is for |
|---|---|---|
| `NOPLOOP` | a sled with no redirect at all | CONTROL — must reproduce the golden law (`B + 4`, gap 4 at w0 when B-limited) |
| `JMPLOOP` | `EB xx` short JMP (redirect, **no bus cycle of its own**) | C3 vs C1: does a non-IRET redirect pay the floor? |
| `RETLOOP` | `C3` near RET (redirect **after** a stack read) | the IRET's shape without the IRET |
| any of the above at **w1/w2/w3** | a longer fetch is more likely to be in flight when the redirect commits | C1 vs C1′: the phase where the restart is DELAYED |

**Predictions, per candidate, written before the capture:**

* **P1 (C1 — the landed reading).**  On `JMPLOOP` and `RETLOOP`, every
  acknowledge whose preceding CODE fetch has `L = 4` opens at `F1 + 6`, and at
  `L ≥ 5` at `F1 + L + 1`.  On `NOPLOOP`, B-limited acknowledges open at the
  back-to-back slot (`F1 + 4` at w0) — **no floor**.
* **P2 (C1′ — the redirect-commit reading).**  Identical to P1 **except** in the
  cells where the refilling fetch's T1 is later than the flush strobe's usual
  offset: there the acknowledge opens EARLIER than `F1 + 6`, tracking the flush
  instead of the fetch.  *This is the only cell that separates C1 from C1′, and
  it is the whole reason the cell needs waits.*
* **P3 (C3 — IRET-specific).**  `JMPLOOP` and `RETLOOP` show **gap 4** at w0,
  i.e. the same as `NOPLOOP` and 2 clocks earlier than the banked post-IRET
  population.  If this is what the capture shows, **C1 is REFUTED, the `sim/`
  landing is REVERTED, and H1 is re-opened as a per-redirect question** — the
  landing is not defended against its own falsifier.
* **P4 (rig integrity).**  The `NOPLOOP` control at w0 reproduces the model
  cell for cell (the model is 200/200 on the golden equivalents).  A control
  that does not reproduce is a rig finding and STOPS the cell.

*Falsifier for the landed law*: any acknowledge, at any wait level, whose
recognition boundary follows a redirect and which opens at a clock other than
`max(F1 + 6, F1 + L + 1)`; or a redirect whose restarted prefetch is delayed and
whose acknowledge does not move with it.
