## §1 WHAT IS BEING LANDED, AND WHY IT IS ONE WIRE

§68.4 measured and discriminated H1a and did **not** land it.  The carried
disposition is taken here verbatim: **the arm becomes "the EU's
interrupt-entry microcode started", published by the EU and consumed by the
BIU — one wire, no address match, strictly generalising today's arm.**

**THE ENTRY FUNNEL IS ONE PLACE, AND THE ROM SAYS SO.**  Read off
`./sim/v30sim disasm docs/V20BITS.TXT`, the interrupt-entry routine is the
page-7 block `111.0001?000.xx` (`01EC`…) — the `?` is the ROM's own statement
that `INT` (far target 2) and `INTEM` (far target 3) are the SAME rows.  The
only door into it is the `FARJMP` micro-op, and **every** entry in the part
goes through that door:

| site | entry |
|---|---|
| `0105` | `CC` — `INT 3` |
| `0108` | `CD` — `INT n` |
| `010F` | `CE` — `INTO` |
| `0195` | `F6`/`F7` group — the DIVIDE trap |
| `01A9` | `IDIV2` — the divide trap's second site |
| `0283` | `62` — `CHKIND`/`BOUND` |
| `01D9` | the hardware `[-00-]` row 0 — **BRK / TF** |
| `01DB` | the hardware `[-00-]` row 2 — **NMI** |
| `01DF` | the hardware `02` bank A tail — **the INTA vector fetch** |
| `01E3` | the hardware `02` bank B tail — **the INTA vector fetch** |
| `0349`, `0401` | `0F FF` / 8080 `ED` — `BRKEM`, via `FARJMP INTEM` (the same rows) |

**THE GENERALISATION IS STRICT, AND IT IS PROVED ON THE ROM, NOT ASSUMED.**
There are exactly **three** `[-05-]` (INTA) rows in the whole ROM — `01DC`,
`01E0`, `01E2` — and all three sit inside blocks that terminate in
`FARJMP INT` (`01DF`, `01E3`).  No `FLUSH` row lies between an INTA row and its
block's `FARJMP`, so the only two consumers of the arm (`flush()`'s re-arm and
`boundary_no_pop()`'s spend) see it in the same order under either condition.
**Every case armed by the INTA condition today is armed by the new one, and the
INTA condition is therefore REPLACED, not supplemented.**

**THE SHAPE, per engine:**

* `sim/` — `exec_impl.h`'s `op.is_farjmp()` branch (the one place the micro-PC
  is loaded from a far target) publishes the entry to the BIU; `biu_timed.cpp`
  arms `bnd_pending_` there instead of in `inta_read()`.
* `hdl/rtl/ucore/` — a new EU output `eu_int_entry`, built exactly like
  `eu_susp` (`row_acts_ok && e_farjmp && <target is the entry block>`), consumed
  by `v30u_biu.sv` in place of `eu_post && (eu_bs == BS_INTA)`.  The
  `eu_bnd_take` / `eu_bnd_post` pair is the precedent for the direction of the
  wire.

**NO NEW STATE IS EXPECTED.**  The arm is an INPUT to the BIU's existing
`bnd_pending` flop and a combinational decode of a row the EU already has
registered.  If a publishing flop turns out to be needed it is SS-mapped and
`SS_VERSION` bumped per precedent, and that is reported as a deviation.

## §2 THE BAR, REWRITTEN — WHAT CHANGED AGAINST §3 AND WHY

§3 of `sm3_s7_prereg_2026-08-04.md` required, for the AUTHORISING outcome:

> `>= 95 %` of `swint-entry`-shaped first acknowledges FLOORED **at every wait
> level 0-3**, AND `>= 95 %` of `iret-only`-shaped ones UNFLOORED.

**§68.4 BOOKED THAT BAR AS DEFECTIVE, and this is the correction it demands.**
The defect is in the CONTROL half and it is structural, not statistical: the
floor is `max(F1 + 6, F1 + L + 1)`, and at `w >= 1` the restart's `L` is 5, 6, 7,
so `L + 1` is 6, 7, 8 and **the floor COLLAPSES ONTO `L + 1`**.  At `w >= 1`
there is no clock at which an acknowledge can sit that distinguishes floored
from unfloored — *"unfloored" is inexpressible in the coordinate*.  Both
engines already reproduce the chip exactly at w1/w2/w3 with an INTA-only arm,
which is the positive control saying the `L + 1` term is structural and carries
no information about the entry.

**THREE CHANGES, EACH NAMED:**

1. **The discriminating regime is w0 ALONE.**  w1-w3 are reported and are
   must-not-move, but they are not evidence for or against the arm.
2. **The statistic is the MINIMUM, not a proportion.**  A floor is a minimum;
   §68.4 says so and refuses to re-read the old bar onto it after the fact,
   which is why the re-read happens HERE, before the landing, in a committed
   file.
3. **The bar is now on the ENGINE LEG, not on the chip leg.**  §3's bar was
   written to decide *what the chip does*; that question is CLOSED by §68.4
   (chip `swintnext` w0 is 30/30 floored, `iretnext` w0 is 25/30 unfloored at
   the minimum).  What this sitting must show is that the ENGINES reproduce it,
   so the bar is scored by `sm3_h1_cell.py score`, which pairs each capture's
   acknowledges with the engine's.

Nothing else in §3 is re-read.  **Its full must-not-move ladder stands
unchanged** and is restated in §6.

## §3 THE AUTHORISING LEG — the h1a cell, `sm3_h1_cell.py score --hold 300`

The cell's captures are the SELECTING population (they chose the mechanism);
§5's bank is the disjoint validating one.  Baselines below were re-measured on
HEAD `587d523637` before any source was touched.

### §3.1 The `swintnext` leg at w0 — **THE AUTHORISING OUTCOME**

| | baseline (HEAD) | **REGISTERED after the landing** |
|---|---|---|
| `ord1  swintnext w0` agreement, `sim` | **0 / 30** | **30 / 30** (authorising floor: `>= 29 / 30`) |
| `ord1  swintnext w0` agreement, `ucore` | **0 / 30** | **30 / 30** (authorising floor: `>= 29 / 30`) |

**THE SLACK ARGUMENT, and it is why 30/30 is claimed rather than 29/30.**  The
cell is fully deterministic — one image per variant, one assert delay per cell,
no randomness anywhere — and the chip's 30 `swintnext` w0 cells are
**identical in the observable**: `L4 -> gap 6`, 30 of 30.  Both engines are
identically wrong on all 30 (`L4 -> gap 4`, 30 of 30).  The mechanism moves the
boundary by exactly the two clocks the floor costs, in every cell or in none; a
partial move would mean the arm is sensitive to the assert delay, which no
candidate on the table predicts and which would itself be a finding.  The
`>= 29 / 30` floor is registered as the authorising bar per the work order, but
**anything other than 30/30 is reported as an anomaly and diagnosed, not
accepted.**

### §3.2 The `iretnext` CONTROL — **MUST NOT MOVE**

`iretnext` plants its frames and reaches the IRET with **no entry of any kind**
behind ordinal 1, so the entry-generic arm must leave it exactly where it is.

### §3.4 The FALSIFIER

* an entry-shaped lead-in (`swint-entry`) whose following acknowledge is
  UNFLOORED on silicon at w0, or
* a floored acknowledge at w0 with **no** entry and **no** INTA behind it,

falsifies the arm as landed.  A `iretnext` control leg that MOVES falsifies it
too: it would mean the new condition is firing where no entry ran.

## §4 THE §64.2 BANKED SEEDS — the two mechanically-re-entry-shaped ones

`mc1/2672` (vector `0x00000`) and `mc1/356` (vector `0x0000c`) are re-entries in
every mechanical sense — entry, handler, IRET, flush, restarted prefetch, and
the acknowledge at that prefetch's index 2 — and are called ordinal 1 only
because their entry was announced by a vector read.  §64.2: *"Both engines put
the acknowledge at gap 4 and are wrong by exactly the 2 clocks the floor
costs."*

**REGISTERED MOVEMENT**, per seed, per engine, measured as
`timed_fuzz --details` / the per-seed report record:

* the seed's `first_bad` row index must move **LATER**, or its verdict must
  IMPROVE.  Either is the expected movement; **neither may get WORSE.**
* `mc2/3821` is **excluded by name**: §64.2 books it as the 8080 landing-pad
  class (§63.5 class A) and both engines diverge long before its acknowledge.
  It is not evidence for or against this landing and is reported as unchanged.

**THE PROTECTED EIGHT ARE THE CONTROL INSIDE THE BANK.**  §64.2's population
census found 12 EVT first acknowledges with both `prev = CODE L = 4` and an
entry behind them; **eight sit at gap 4, BELOW the floor.**  They are protected
by the SPEND rule — the floor is stamped by the restarted prefetch and spent by
the first pop, and those eight have pops between the entry's return and the
acknowledge.  **REGISTERED: none of the eight may become floored.**  If the
landing floors them, the arm is too wide and it is REVERTED, not defended.

## §5 THE FUZZ COLUMNS — direction, floor, and the DISJOINT VALIDATION

**THE VALIDATION-RULE REQUIREMENT, STATED EXPLICITLY (§64.1).**  The
`sm3-h1acell` captures SELECTED this mechanism — they are what refuted A2 and
what the arm is being written from.  **They may not also validate it.**  The
3,242-seed fuzz bank shares no capture with them (different generator,
different sitting, different bitstream) and is therefore the DISJOINT
VALIDATION POPULATION.  §4's two named seeds are members of it.

### §5.1 The REGISTERED column — **A PROVABLE NO-OP, registered as EQUALITY**

The arm has exactly two consumers: `flush()`'s re-arm (which only sets
`bnd_arm_` / `bnd_floor_`) and `boundary_no_pop()` (which reads the floor and
suspends).  `boundary_no_pop()` is reached only through `at_fire_boundary()`,
i.e. only when a pin event is being replayed; in the ucore the equivalent
`eu_bnd_take` is `at_bnd && irq_any && …`.  **The 1,702-seed REGISTERED
population carries no `evt` axis at all**, so the arm has no reader there and
the change cannot be observed.

| | registered |
|---|---|
| `timed_fuzz --core sim` REGISTERED | **EXACTLY 1,272 / 1,702** |
| `timed_fuzz --core ucore` REGISTERED | **EXACTLY 1,490 / 1,702** |

Not `>=`.  **Any movement at all in this column is a DEFECT** — it says the new
condition is firing somewhere the old one did not and reaching a reader it
should not have.

### §5.2 The EVT column — the only population that can move

Direction: **the entry-generic arm can only ADD floors**, and only where a
non-INTA entry (a software `INT`/`INT3`/`INTO`, a divide or `BOUND` trap, or an
NMI) precedes a later recognition boundary with no INTA between them.

**THE BOUND, from §64.2's chip-side census, and it is small**: of 771 first
acknowledges in the EVT bank, **12** have an entry behind them.  Two are
floored on the chip (§4's pair), eight are protected by the spend rule, one is
the 8080 class, one sits at gap 161.  Non-first acknowledges cannot move: an
INTA is already behind them and they are already armed.  The one class the
census does not bound is an NMI entry followed by a SECOND recognition in the
same seed, because `sm3_ackgeom`'s ordinal counts INTA cycles and an NMI has
none; that class is measured after the landing and reported.
