## §1 THE MECHANISM — **B-1 IS M6, AND M6 IS REFUTED BY ITS OWN CENSUS**

### §1.1 The clock, derived

`s10-hltsweep-w0 HLT.INT idx 3`, model clocks (`V30SIM_EVALTRACE`; `ET N` is
the eval at the END of clock `N`, display at `N + 1`; capture row = clock − 7):

| clock | what happens |
|---|---|
| 6…9 | the running `CODE` cycle, `T1`…`T4` |
| end of 8 | its COMPLETION eval — `.O`, refused, `halted_` |
| 9 | `T4`; the fetch's bytes are pushed, `pf_land_from_ = pf_land_to_ = 10` |
| 10 | the bus is free; **for `d ≥ 4` the HALT display takes the register here** (`H = 10`, golden row 3) |
| **end of 10** | the first idle eval — **`.M`, refused by M6** |
| end of 11 | granted; display at clock 12 = **row 5** |

**The golden's wake `CODE` display is row 4 = clock 11**, i.e. granted at the
end of clock 10 — the eval M6 refuses.  §79.G's *"display at `H + 1`"* is that
clock exactly, and it is the same clock the HALT announcement would have taken.

### §1.2 Why M6 is the only thing there, and why that is not a coincidence

`no_eval_` (M2r) already kills the clock after any completion eval.  **Under
waits the completion eval is at `T4`, so `no_eval_ = T4 + 1` and M6's window is
covered by construction — M6 can only ever bite at w0**, where the completion
eval is at `T3` and `no_eval_ = T4`.  That is why family B is a w0-only
residue: it is not a wait-dependent law, it is the one wait level at which M6
is reachable at all.

### FIRING THAT IS OBSERVABLE AGAINST SILICON IS A FIRING SILICON DOES NOT MAKE**

**M6's own authorising populations no longer reach the branch.**  What
subsumes it is `no_eval_`: 12.1's measured consequence — *"the earliest eval
that may resume a prefetch after a pushing fetch is `T4+2`, and that fetch's
`T1` opens at `T4+4`, at every wait level"* — is produced by M2r's eval
geometry alone at every wait level above zero, and at w0 the only reachable
firings are the ones silicon contradicts.  **This is not a claim that the T2b
measurement was wrong.  It is the claim that the rule it authorised is now
redundant where it agrees and wrong where it does not.**
