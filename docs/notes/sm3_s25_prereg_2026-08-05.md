## A. THE `ucore` BRK/TF LEG

### A.0 What is already settled and is NOT re-opened

* The law (§84.3 / §85.2b): **one arm bit; at every instruction boundary the
  machine first TAKES the trap if the arm is set and then SAMPLES TF into it; a
  prefix byte ends a boundary; the arm does not see a TF that rose fewer than
  THREE clocks before the sample.** The floor is MEASURED at 3 (§85.2b:
  121,890 rows, 0 row-diffs at 3, ≥ 11,032 at every other value in [1,7]).
* §84.7's pure-gate ucore spec is **REFUTED** (§85.3: the saturated controls
  `popfmemr`/`popfmul` wait one more boundary at N = 13 and 25, so no `N` fits
  a gate). The arm is a **real flop with take-then-sample semantics**.
* §85.2a's instrument correction: the model's sample instant is **exactly one
  clock past the successor's `QS = 1` opcode pop**, 459/459 + 70/70 after the
  fix, 0 violators over 2,900 boundaries on TF-clear captures.

### A.1 THE RENDERING, registered before it is written

Five flops, no opcode named anywhere, nothing fitted:

1. **`brk_p[2:0]`** — `psw[FBRK]` through the same flops, frozen at the same
   instant and shifted in the same block (g), as `ie_p`. The floor term is
   **`brk_seen = psw[FBRK] && brk_p[2]`** — "TF up NOW and up three clocks
   ago", `ie_p`'s sentence one bit over.
2. **`brk_arm`** — THE ARM. One flop that crosses boundaries.
3. **`brk_smp`** — the sample instant, one clock past the opcode pop
   (A.2 below). This is the ONLY flop §85.3's spec did not name, and A.2 is
   the trap-free test that decides whether it exists at all.

and the two events:

* **SAMPLE**: on the clock after an **F pop** — `q_pop && q_ripe && q_first`,
  which is exactly the pop the `QS = 1` pins announce. `brk_arm <= brk_seen`.
  **This needs NO prefix special case**: a prefix retires with its own F pop
  (`prefix_retire()` → `pop_is_first`), so "a prefix byte ends an instruction
  boundary" is already what the pop stream says. §85.3's "the sampling
  boundaries are the retire boundaries **and** the prefix hand-over" is ONE
  predicate here, not two.
* **TAKE**: `brk_fire = at_bnd && brk_arm`, on the EXISTING boundary wire, and
  the arm is cleared at the four sites that already reach `S_IRQ_D`. The entry
  is the existing vector-1 door: `S_IRQ_D` routes `7.00.0` (CONST 1) where it
  routes `7.00.2` for NMI and `7.02.0` for INTA. `FBRK` is already cleared on
  entry by `I_CITF`.
* **The tie**: an external recognition WINS over the trap at the same boundary,
  and the arm is cleared either way. This is the C++'s
  `at_fire_boundary() = ext_fire() || brk_take_` /
  `brk_fired_ = brk_take_ && !ext_fire()` rendered edge for edge. It is
  INHERITED, not measured — see A.6.

### A.2 THE ONE FREE COORDINATE, AND ITS TRAP-FREE ACCEPTANCE TEST

The law fixes the floor at 3 clocks **from the rise to the sample instant**.
It does not, by itself, say which RTL clock the sample instant is. §85.2a
measured that instant in the model — **one past the successor's `QS = 1`
pop** — and that measurement is engine-vs-chip with no trap in the loop.

**REGISTERED, and decided BEFORE the trap is scored anywhere:** the ucore's
sample instant must satisfy the SAME statistic on the SAME population —

> **(A-0)** On the six TF-clear control captures of the s24 cell
> (`notfnone` / `notfclc` / `iretnotf` × w0, w3), every one of the ucore's
> arm-sample clocks is **exactly one past an emitted `QS = 1` row**, and the
> count of sample clocks equals the count of `QS = 1` rows (window edges
> excluded). **Bar: 0 violators.**

If (A-0) fails, the rendering is wrong and is fixed before anything else is
run; the floor tap stays at `brk_p[2]` in every case. **Trading the tap depth
against the instant to make a trap score come out is EXPLICITLY FORBIDDEN by
this registration.**

### A.6 KNOWN DIVERGENCES FROM THE MODEL, BOOKED IN ADVANCE

1. **`irq_shadow` is not applied to the trap.** The model does not shadow the
   BRK take behind a segment-register load; the ucore will not either, because
   the instruction is to land edge for edge. Real silicon is documented to
   inhibit the single-step trap after a `MOV SS,r` exactly as it inhibits
   `INT`. **UNMEASURED, and this tree has no cell for it.** *Falsifier*: a
   capture in which a TF trap is taken at the boundary after a segment-register
   load.
2. **`eu_bnd_post` gains an `irq_take` term.** The prefetcher suspend belongs
   to the recognition that PAYS the IE floor; the trap never pays it (it is not
   IE-gated, exactly as NMI is not, and `eu_bnd_post` already excludes NMI for
   that reason). The added term is provably inert for every existing path,
   because `eu_bnd_post` is only ever consumed as `eu_bnd_take && eu_bnd_post`
   and the old `eu_bnd_take` (`at_bnd && irq_take`) already implied `irq_take`.
   The A.4 ladder is what says so in numbers.
3. **`ulockstep` is VACUOUS for this trap** and is run as a non-regression
   check only: a single-instruction case has no successor boundary to trap at,
   and the case runner leaves the arm off in both engines. The cross-engine
   proof is A.5 — the same 30 captures scored against both engines — plus the
   seed-level agreement of A.3.

## B. THE `TAIL_EXTRA` SURVEY — **SURVEY, NOT A LANDING**

28 shared REGISTERED seeds. `s15_census`'s definition: the cycle SEQUENCES
agree over the whole common prefix AND the times agree, and one side launched
MORE bus cycles than the other — the engines run on past where the chip stops.

**Deliverable**: the per-seed tail geometry (where the chip stops, what the
extra cycles are, what precedes the stop), the invariant, ranked candidates
against the settled ledger (M4 / M19 / SUSP / F54's cancellation-law
neighbours), and a discriminating cell spec if one is needed.

**Registered disposition rule**: a landing happens **only** if ONE mechanism
covers the whole population and pre-registers cleanly, and it lands in `sim/`
first because the family is shared. Anything less is reported as a survey and
the number that says why is printed. §84.6's B2a precedent governs: a landing
sold as "closes the family" that the evidence supports on 1-2 seeds is not
taken.
