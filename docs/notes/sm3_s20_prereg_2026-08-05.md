### A.1 THE LAW, WHICH IS ALREADY WRITTEN DOWN

> **F51.**  A HALT pseudo-cycle has NO DATA PHASE.  After its address phase it
> **drives nothing**, and the pads hold whatever was last put on them.

`v30u_biu.sv` renders the second clause as a **DRIVE**:
`halt_hold = r_run && r_cur_halt` keeps `ad_oe_addr` asserted for the WHOLE
pseudo-cycle and republishes `r_cur_addr` on every clock of it.  Where no
announcement intervenes, "hold" and "re-drive" are the same value by
construction — which is why 168,997 `w0` goldens cannot see the difference.
They differ exactly when a multi-clock announcement takes the pads in between
and is then WITHDRAWN, and that is the 35-cell class.

`tb_v30_core.sv`'s composer excludes a HALT-typed cycle from `core_ps_drive`,
so the DEFAULT TB floats those clocks and scores them green **on the
instrument's authority, not the core's** (`standing_gates.md` meta-finding #5).
`system_large` keys on the core's own `AD_OE` port and does not, which is the
whole 273-vs-268 split.

### A.2 THE CHANGE, REGISTERED AS TEXT BEFORE IT IS MADE

`hdl/rtl/ucore/v30u_biu.sv`, **two terms, no new state, no new constant, no
per-case anything**:

1. the HALT's address one-shot is bounded to its ADDRESS PHASE —
   `r_run && r_cur_halt && (r_ts == TS_T1)` instead of `r_run && r_cur_halt`.
   Its `ad_oe_addr` contribution is then wholly subsumed by the existing
   `(r_run && (r_ts == TS_T1))` term, and its `ad_o` branch keeps the HALT's T1
   publishing `r_cur_addr` exactly as today.  The wire is RENAMED to
   `halt_addr` because `halt_hold` would then be a false name; it has no
   consumer outside one `$display`.
2. `ad_oe_ps` gains `!r_cur_halt` on its body branch.  Without it, dropping (1)
   merely swaps one drive for another — the PS/data drive would take the pads
   over at the HALT's T2 — and F51 says a HALT drives NOTHING after its address
   phase.  `ad_oe_data` already carries `!r_cur_halt`.

Net effect: at a HALT pseudo-cycle's T2/T3/Tw/T4 with no display, all three
enables are LOW, `AD` floats, and what the pads show is **retention** — which
`system_large` and the fabric already model, and which `tb_v30_core`'s `hold`
already renders.

## §B H7 — THE REPETITION RE-CAPTURE

### B.0 WHAT IS OPEN, IN ONE PARAGRAPH

Over the 208 banked pin-1 (NMI) seeds the chip's vector-read gap `V − A` has a
floor of **12** on exactly **30** seeds; both engines floor at **13** and the
30 are exactly the seeds they miss (§63.2).  Every DIRECTED board population
built since — §63.3's delay sweep, §65.1's primed/dry cell (160 captures),
§68.5's ten-opcode cell (640 captures), §72's IE cell — floors at **13**, with
**zero** captures below 12 anywhere.  The eliminated axes are wait class, rig
delay, bus owner at `A`, queue occupancy at `A`, queue primed vs dry, the
composition of the window `[A, V)`, and the instruction at the boundary.
The one live lead §68.5 left is that the floor population is **BANK-ASSOCIATED**
— `mc2` **0 of 75**, `mc1` **26 of 85**, `t30-raw` **4 of 33** — which was
booked and not chased.

**Re-measured board-free at the top of this sitting and unchanged:**
`sm3_nmigeom --pin 1` over 208 seeds → gap histogram `12 : 30, 13 : 18, …`,
`arm` found 208/208, and the 30 partition **mc1 26 / t30-raw 4 / mc2 0**.

**THE SURVIVING HYPOTHESIS.**  The 12s are a **capture-era sub-clock artifact**
— the electrical margin of the rig's synchronous pin assert against the chip's
internal sampling instant, sensitive to session conditions and possibly to the
pre-`div_guard` rig era.  It is the only axis every directed cell has held
CONSTANT, because every directed cell was captured in one session on one rig
state.  **THE DISCRIMINATOR IS REPETITION**, and it needs no new geometry.

### B.1 THE CELL

`sw/sm3_h7_repeat.py`, built for this and modelled on `inv1_recapture.py`:

* **the population is DERIVED, never a list** — every banked seed with
  `evt.pin == 1` whose measured `gap == 12`, computed by `sm3_nmigeom.one`'s
  own code path, so the tool and the finding cannot drift apart;
* per seed the image is REGENERATED from `(cid, k, ov)` and **HASH-CHECKED**
  against the banked `image_sha256` — GEN-DRIFT is a hard skip, never a silent
  capture;
* **N = 10 repetitions** per seed at the banked directive (`delay`, `hold = 2`,
  `pin = 1`) and the banked wait legs, socket only;
* **the pin-0 control**: 10 seeds from the banked INT population, 3 reps each,
  whose INTA1 gaps must reproduce their banked values EXACTLY.  This is the
  control that says the rig and the `A` derivation are sound in THIS session.
  It is not a result; if it moves, nothing else in §B is readable;
* full per-clock rows + `sha256` retained per repetition under
  `sw/testdata/sm3-h7rep/`; `div_guard` PINNED and recorded at both ends;
  `board_idle()` at the close; 15 consecutive transport errors STOP.
