## 1.  P4′ — THE GHOST-READ ADDRESS SPACE (M7).  **STOP AND BOOK.  NOT LANDED.**

### 1.1  The seats — 4, re-derived (families E2 and C3)

| seat | family | fork row | dispatched successor at the fork | chip | ucore |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2c/410028` | C3 | 2994 | `ed` = `IN AX,DX` | **IOR** | MEMR |
| `fz2e/520066` | E2 | 1249 | `ec` = `IN AL,DX` | **IOR** | MEMR |
| `fz2e/527055` | E2 | 655 | `6d` = `INM`/`INS` | **IOR** | MEMR |
| `fz2e/528030` | E2 | 423 | `ec` = `IN AL,DX` | **IOR** | MEMR |

The fourth E2 seed (`fz2e/512038`) is a HALT/INTA seed and is **not** M7.

### 1.2  The law, confirmed and stated as ONE predicate

> The ghost read's announced **space** is the space of the decode standing at
> the announcement, taken only when that decode's access runs in the **same
> direction** as the cycle.  A successor whose I/O access is a WRITE (`6F`,
> `EE`/`EF`, `E6`/`E7`) cannot lend its space to a READ cycle, and the
> announcement stays `MEMR` — which is the `6F` control the wave-2 census
> named.

In `v30u_eu.sv` that decode is already published: `eu_bs` is combinational off
the standing row (`row_is_read ? (acc_io ? BS_IOR : BS_MEMR) : …`).  The BIU
latches it once, at the post (`rq_bs[rq_n[0]] = eu_bs`), 3–7 clocks early.

### 1.3  WHY IT CANNOT BE LANDED IN THE BIU — measured, per seat

Instrumented `v30u_biu.sv` with a temporary per-CE-clock trace (`+bstrace`,
removed before this document was written) and replayed each seat on `tb_sys`;
the trace aligns to the banked rows **300/300 on `ts`+`bs`** in every seat.

| seat | opcode POP row | ghost DISPLAY row | `eu_bs` at the display | at the T1 | first clock `eu_bs == IOR` |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2c/410028` | 2994 | **2994** | MEMW | MEMW | 2996 = pop **+2** |
| `fz2e/528030` | 423 | **423** | MEMW | MEMW | 425 = pop **+2** |
| `fz2e/527055` | 655 | **655** | MEMW | MEMW | 657 = pop **+2** |
| `fz2e/520066` | 1246 | 1249 | **IOR** | **IOR** | 1248 = pop **+2** |

The ucore's EU publishes the successor's `eu_bs` at **pop + 2** without
exception.  On three of the four seats the announcement silicon is scoring is at
**pop + 0**.  So:

* a live sample of `eu_bs` at the display, or at the T1, closes **`fz2e/520066`
  only** — the seat where the bus happened to be busy for three extra clocks —
  and is inert on the other three.  Landing a law whose input is late is not a
  partial close of the mechanism; it is a close of the seats where the latency
  happens not to bite;
* the ONLY carrier in the BIU at pop+0 is `q_byte` — the opcode byte being
  popped — i.e. **a per-`q_byte` opcode table.  That is the brief's own STOP
  condition and it is taken.**

### 1.4  The finding this produces, booked

The one-clock part of the gap is **structural and general**, not 8F's: the
8086/V30 `QS` pins report the queue operation of the **previous** clock, and
the ucore publishes `qs` LIVE at its own pop (`v30u_biu.sv`, `assign qs = … :
pop_now ? …`).  For the pin columns to match — and they do, 300/300 in every
window above — the ucore's pop must sit **one clock later on the pin timeline
than silicon's**.  Every decode-derived rail in this core is therefore one
clock late relative to silicon *as seen from the bus*, and it is invisible
everywhere the consumer is also a decode.  It becomes visible exactly here,
where a **bus announcement** meets a **decode**.

**Owner: `v30u_eu.sv` (not this territory).**  The shape that would work is an
EU rail published at the pop clock off the loader's own PLA3 lookup of the byte
it is popping — "the instruction I am taking now makes an I/O read" — which the
loader already computes combinationally one stage later.  Falsifier for that
future landing: any ghost-read post whose successor is an I/O **write**
(`6F`/`EE`/`EF`/`E6`/`E7`) must stay `MEMR`; `6F` versus `6D` is the control and
it is already in the corpus.

**No RTL is written for P4′ in this landing.**

## 2.  P5′ — THE CS RETARGET (M5).  **HALF LANDED, HALF BOOKED.**

All six seats are family **D3**, all six dispatch `8E /1` at the fork.  The two
halves are different mechanisms and the measurement separates them cleanly.

Current RTL (`v30u_biu.sv:918`):

```
wire [15:0] cmt_cs_live = {flush_cs[15:9], flush_cs[8] & flush_cs_old[8], flush_cs[7:0]};
wire cmt_cs_retarget = flush_cs_we && r_cmt_valid && r_cmt_fetch;
```

### 2.1  (b) THE SINGLE-CLOCK RETARGET — 2 seats — **THIS IS THE LANDING**

`flush_cs_we` is asserted for exactly ONE clock and it meets a fetch
announcement that is already standing.  Both seats, exact, on all 16 bits:

| seat | display row | `flush_cs` (new) | `flush_cs_old` | `cmt_prev_fp` | chip's fetch address | implied chip CS | `new & old` | ucore (bit-8 rule) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2c/406023` | 1230 | `59c9` | `f171` | `b536` | `5c946` | **`5141`** | **`5141`** ✓ | `59c9` → `651c6` ✗ |
| `fz2e/527051` | 156 | `cddb` | `fe6a` | `9a66` | `d5f06` | **`cc4a`** | **`cc4a`** ✓ | `ccdb` → `d6816` ✗ |

**THE LAW:** the segment-register read the prefetch adder sees *while the
register is being written* is the **wired AND of the old and the new value** —
a precharged read bus with both the held cell and the write driver pulling on
it.  One expression, 16 bits, no special case:

```
wire [15:0] cmt_cs_live = flush_cs & flush_cs_old;
```

This **DELETES** a fitted one-bit special case (`CS[8]` alone) and its
four-row socket table.  Relationship to that 2026-08-06 socket tranche, stated
honestly: the tranche varied **only CS[8]**, and **its `old` CS value is not
recorded in the comment or anywhere in the tree** (grepped).  With an `old` that
carries the other bits the tranche's four rows are consistent with the 16-bit
AND; with an `old` that does not, they are not.  **The tranche therefore cannot
adjudicate between the two laws, and is not being quoted as if it could.**  What
adjudicates is two silicon captures agreeing with the AND on all sixteen bits,
twice.

**Falsifier for the AND:** any capture in which the retargeted fetch address
implies a CS with a bit SET that is clear in `flush_cs` or clear in
`flush_cs_old`.

### 2.2  (a) THE STALLED CS-WRITE ROW — 4 seats — **BOOKED, NOT LANDED**

`flush_cs_we` is asserted as a **LEVEL** for the whole stall of the CS-writing
row, and `flush_cs` during that level is **not the value that will be written**:

| seat | display row | `we` level | `flush_cs` during the level | value actually written | `flush_cs_old` | chip's fetch address | implied chip CS | ucore |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `fz2e/520062` | 700 | 691→702 | `2774` | `a243` | `5cdb` | `69df8` | **`5cdb` = OLD** | `33788` |
| `fz2e/528008` | 628 | …→630 | `3b1f` | `3e05` | `0ecc` | `1468e` | **`0ecc` = OLD** | `3fbbe` |
| `fz2e/532012` | 328 | 320→330 | `bd85` | `64c3` | `b674` | `b8114` | **`b674` = OLD** | `be224` |
| `fz2e/533028` | 881 | 870→883 | `7664` (the DISPLACEMENT) | `ccac` | `12fe` | `22a58` | **`12fe` = OLD** | `860b8` |

The chip does **not** retarget on any of these, and it commits the fetch itself
from the CS the register **holds** — which is exactly the law
`v30u_biu.sv:907-909` already states (*"a fetch whose T1 is already running is
not retargeted"*, generalised: **the register is written once, and nothing
happens on the clocks where it is not**).  In the ucore two consumers act on the
level instead: `fetch_lin = {flush_cs, …}` (`:1971`) and the retarget itself.

**Why it is not landed here.**  `flush_cs_we` is `wr_cs1` — `e_have1 && (the
row's destination is CS)` — the ROW'S INTENT, held across its stall, and
`flush_cs` is `s1_now`, the source as it currently stands.  Distinguishing "the
register is written **this** clock" from "a row that will write it is stalled"
is **not available in the BIU combinationally**, and the two flop-free
candidates both fail:

* `flush_cs != flush_cs_old` — true throughout the stall;
* the BIU's own `r_cs_r` — it observes the change one clock LATE, and (b) needs
  the answer on the display clock itself.

A registered edge detector would need a flop, and a flop needs a save-state
address, and **`ss_lint` must stay UNMOVED across this landing** (single-writer
territory).  **Owner: `v30u_eu.sv`** — qualify `flush_cs_we` by the row actually
committing, so the BIU sees the one-clock write the die performs.  Falsifier for
that future landing: the four seats above must take the OLD CS, and
`fz2c/406023` / `fz2e/527051` must keep taking `new & old`.

⚠ Landing (b) alone will **change** the (a) seats' (already wrong) addresses,
because `cmt_cs_live` feeds them too.  They stay failures; their `first_bad_row`
is predicted **unchanged** (the divergence is at the same announcement row) and
that is a registered bar, not a hope.
