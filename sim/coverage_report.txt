       unconditional `CTL FARJMP`, which has no delay slot

       sequence retires (or jumps away) before reaching them.  Criterion
       (the same one used at S3): every LATER row of the same bank is also
       dead -- 93/93 satisfy it, and 93 are not a bank's row 0.
